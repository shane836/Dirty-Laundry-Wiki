## CHARACTER 6: PAMELA "PAM" WORTHINGTON III | The Divorcee

**Tagline:** *Chasing husband number two while haunted by the death of husband number one—or does she haunt it?*

**Era:** Mid-forties. Resort wear trying very hard. Jewelry announcing recent alimony.

**Public Persona:** Came to "find herself" but really to find rich husband #2. Friendly, slightly desperate, drinks too much, laughs too loud. Says "I'm just here to have fun!" with an edge of sadness underneath.

**Real Story:** Serious anger management issues when drinking. Sober: charming, witty, thoughtful. Past four cocktails: volatile, confrontational, frightening. Blackout episodes increasingly frequent. Trigger: comments about her age, looks, or worth. 

First husband Gerald Worthington II died at lake house in Tahoe four years ago. Officially: slipped on dock after drinking, hit head, drowned. Unofficially: they'd been fighting all evening, neighbors heard screaming, bruising inconsistent with simple fall. Worthington lawyers closed investigation quietly. Did Pam push Gerald? She genuinely doesn't know—she was blackout drunk and hasn't filled that gap in two years of sobriety attempts.

**Connection to Richie:** Dinner date night before murder. Drinking heavily. Richie made comment about her age ("well-preserved for a woman your age"). She flew into rage. Next evening at after-party, together again, another conflict. Pam blacked out around 9:45 PM. 

BIG REVEAL: Woke up near laundry area at 11:30 PM. Found a vial with chemical residue in her hand. Doesn't remember anything. Panicking all day wondering if she killed him. Has confided in Gloria (who covered for her), terrified of police investigation.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Gloria Reyes**: Genuine best friend. Met at Tampa charity gala. Gloria covers for blackouts—drives her home, makes excuses, holds her hair. Most real relationship either has.
- **Irina Volkov**: Pam went to laundry room looking for aspirin during blackout. Irina may have seen Pam near body. Witness and suspect linked by proximity.
- **Doug Kaminski**: Doug mildly flirting with her as backup plan. She finds him amusing but not husband material.
- **Richie**: Was pursuing him as husband #2. His comment triggered rage.

**Financial Entanglements:**
- Living on alimony from Gerald (approximately $8K monthly, but finite)
- Worthington lawyers keep investigation into Gerald's death closed
- Spending heavily to project wealth she's running out of
- Six-month runway before alimony depletes or comes under scrutiny

**Alibi:**
- **Plausible:** Was at party, spotted by various guests at various times. But for how long? Did she leave? When?
- **Implausible:** This is the point. There is no plausible alibi. Woke up near body with vial in hand. History of violence during blackouts. First husband's death wasn't accident. Evidence is overwhelming—which is exactly why she might be innocent. Too perfect. Too narrative.

**Motive: UNCLEAR** (7/10—depends on consciousness)
- If she did it: drunken manslaughter, repeat killer who doesn't realize it
- If she didn't: carrying guilt for both husbands anyway
- Rage at Richie's comment is documented and witnessed
- Physical evidence circumstantial but damaging
- Pattern suggests capability

**Red Herrings & Layers:**
- Obvious suspect (which may be deflection)
- Genuine victim status (alimony running out, aging out of options)
- Real best friendship with Gloria (complicates narrative)
- Possible trauma response to Gerald's death (blacking out specifically)
- Vial could be planted on drunk woman (by whom? why?)
- Two mysterious deaths four years apart, same circumstances (slipping, alcohol, no memory)

**Secrets:**
- Woke up near Richie's body with vial in hand
- First husband Gerald's death wasn't accident
- Blackout episodes with violent outcomes
- Doesn't remember what she did during critical window
- Running out of money, alimony finite
- Gloria knows about morning after and isn't reporting her
