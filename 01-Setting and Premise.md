# Setting & Premise

## What Is This Game?

**Medium:** A murder mystery party — group cosplay with a loose overarching mystery plot that is slowly revealed over several rounds of play. Think of it as improv meets knives out. Get in character and try and crack the case. Or if you're guilty, lie through your teeth and avoid getting caught.


### How It Works

The game plays out over **4** of improv role play. Each round, players receive a **game card** with 3–4 bullet points telling them what to say, what to look out for, and what information to drop. Between rounds, the host makes announcements that push the story forward — new evidence surfaces, alibis fall apart, twists land.

Players also get a **dossier** at the start of the night — a short character sheet covering who they are, their backstory, their motive, their alibi, and their connections to other characters. The dossier is their bible. The game cards are their script (loosely).

Each character should have just enough information to know what they should do or say, but they have the freedom to act how they'd like and make the character their own.

### Round Structure

1. **Round 1 — Exposition:** Players mingle and introduce their characters. They drop background info and start establishing connections.
2. **Rounds 2–3— Building suspense:** New clues emerge. Alibis get challenged. Twists redirect suspicion. Characters reveal secrets or get caught in lies. The host announces new developments at the start/end of each round.
3. **Final Round — The Reveal:** The killer is unmasked. The host walks through the solution.

## The Game: "Clean Lewks, Dirty $$$: Murder in the Caymans"

### Logline

A group of high-level money launderers, bankers, and shady operators are gathered at a private island resort for an exclusive "best practices in tax evasion" conference. The world's leading offshore tax consultant is found dead — just after announcing he's about to blow the whistle on every tax shelter in the room.

### Theme

**Tropical corruption.** Flashy, sun-drenched, and dangerous. Think pastel suits and dirty money. Everyone's rich, everyone's guilty of something, and nobody's telling the whole truth.

### Setting

A luxury conference hotel in the Grand Caymans. The body was found in a canvas laundry basket after the conference afterparty. The whole event is the victim's creation — an influencer-style, multi-level-marketing pitch for large-scale tax fraud.
