## SECTION B: ROUND 1 — "THE AFTER-PARTY"
### Duration: 45 Minutes

A tight, beat-by-beat plot structure. Specific HOST directions, character beats, and evidence drops woven throughout.

---

### OPENING (0:00–0:05)
### **"Welcome to Heathels Hollow"**

**HOST reads:**

"Welcome to Heathels Hollow. It's 8 PM on a Friday night in Grand Cayman Island. The Tax Optimization Seminar just wrapped its closing dinner. Now it's after-party time in the hotel courtyard.

"Tiki torches are burning. Steel drum music is playing. The pool glows electric blue. Everyone has a cocktail in hand. Everyone's loose, talking loud, laughing too hard. Energy is high.

"You're all here for a reason, and that reason is: you're all guilty of something.

"Richard Rathbone—Richie to his friends, though he doesn't have many—promised a final toast at 9 PM. Something, he said, 'nobody will want to miss.' For now, there's time to mingle. Drink. Be yourself. Or be whoever you're pretending to be.

"The pool is glowing. The music is playing. Richie is nowhere to be seen.

"What do you do?"

---

### THE MINGLE (0:05–0:25)
### **Character Arrivals & Public Personas**

**HOST NOTE:** Allow players to circulate freely in character. All personas are public at this stage—no deep secrets yet. This is where they establish relationships and test each other's reactions. Stagger arrivals for energy and momentum.

#### **VAL** — *Already there, holding court*
Arrives first or is already at the party when players appear. She owns the space. Greeting everyone warmly, champagne in hand, introducing players to each other.

**Key beats:**
- Welcomes each player to "her" hotel
- Name-drops real estate connections
- Mentions Zane casually several times
- Laughs easily, touches people on the arm when she talks
- Avoids Pam and keeps physical distance from her

#### **DEX** — *Behind the bar, in his element*
Working the bar, making drinks, fully locked in. Observant. Witty. Remembers drink orders before people ask. All cocktail small talk—light, charming, professional.

**Key beats:**
- Makes a perfect drink without being asked (reads someone's body language)
- Deflects personal questions with humor
- Keeps moving—never stationary for long
- Mentions Richie ordered vodka earlier: "Already half-drunk before the party started"

#### **MARCO** — *Valet energy, charming, eager*
Technically working valet but pops into the party for quick errands. Charming and eager to please. Works the room like he's trying to sell something.

**Key beats:**
- Mentions parking "a very nice car" for someone (let players wonder whose)
- Asks people about their vehicles
- Friendly with Gloria, awkward with Richie (when Richie appears)
- Stays near the exit—ready to leave if called for parking

#### **ZANE** — *Crypto bro energy, always on the phone*
Arrives talking crypto. On his phone. Name-dropping VCs and portfolio companies. Mentions the importance of his hardware wallet at least twice—like he's making a point.

**Key beats:**
- Distracted, checks phone frequently
- Talks money in casual ways (mentions "seven figures" offhand)
- Close to Val—they exchange knowing looks
- Slightly on edge, though trying to hide it

#### **GLORIA** — *Soccer mom in linen, perfectly composed*
Arrives looking like she came from a PTA meeting: linen dress, blow-out, understated jewelry. Asks about families. Remembers names and uses them. Very maternal, very engaged.

**Key beats:**
- Remembers something specific about each person
- Asks about kids and home life
- Moves gracefully through the room
- Speaks quietly—people have to lean in to hear

#### **PAM** — *Already slightly tipsy, getting drunker*
Arrives and immediately orders "whatever's strongest." Makes jokes about looking for "husband number two." Laughs too loud. Touches people too much.

**Key beats:**
- Orders progressively stronger drinks
- Mentions she and Richie "had some things to work through"
- Gets clingy with other players
- Checks her phone when she thinks no one's looking

#### **DOUG** — *Conspicuously absent*
NOT HERE. Someone will ask. Answer: "Porta potty emergency in Pittsburgh. Some client emergency. He said he'd be back by midnight." Establish him as unreliable, always late, dealing with logistical chaos.

#### **IRINA** — *Visible but in the background, managing staff*
Visible in the background, moving between tables, managing staff. Nods when spoken to. Halting English. Keeps the party running smoothly. Doesn't participate in conversation.

**Key beats:**
- Efficient
- Watchful
- Quiet
- Avoids eye contact with Richie

#### **SUNNY** — *There but forgettable, intentionally*
She's present but unmemorable. Small talk about "enterprise optimization" and "synergistic solutions." Nobody pays attention. That's exactly as she's planned it.

**Key beats:**
- Blends in
- Taking mental notes on everyone
- Responds to questions but doesn't initiate
- Leaves early to observe from the periphery

### THE VIBE SHIFTS (0:25–0:30)
### **Where's Richie?**

**HOST interrupts the mingle:**

"It's 9 PM. Richie was supposed to give his toast five minutes ago. The stage is empty. The microphone is waiting. Nothing.

"Players might joke: 'He's probably finishing the vodka.' Nervous laughter. Val looks annoyed. She checks her watch. Marco hasn't seen him since dinner. Someone suggests he's probably changing into something more dramatic—Richie always has a costume angle.

"Another ten minutes pass. Music plays. Drinks flow. But there's an edge to the room. People keep looking at the stage. Val calls Richie's room phone—no answer. Dex confirms Richie ordered vodka at the bar at 7 PM, sent to his room: 'Standard Rathbone move.'"

**HOST:** "Somewhere between annoyance and concern. The after-party keeps going, but it's like everyone's waiting for permission to have real fun."

---

### THE BODY (0:30–0:40)
### **"He is not breathing"**

**HOST (interrupt whatever's happening):**

"A SCREAM. High. Panicked. From the service area. From the laundry room.

"Irina comes running out, face white, hands shaking. Speaking fast in panicked Russian, then in broken English:

"'Mr. Rathbone. He is... he is in the laundry. He is not... he is not breathing. There is... something on his shirt. Something wrong. Please. Please come.'

"The music stops. Everyone looks at her."

**HOST NOTE:** Sell the panic. Make it visceral. Guide players toward the service area. Describe the scene in specific, disturbing detail:

**The Laundry Room:**

- **The Body:** Richard Rathbone, face-down in a stainless steel commercial laundry basket. Clearly dead. Blue-tinged lips. Discoloration around mouth and nose.
- **Chemical Residue:** Yellowish-white powdery substance on his shirt collar and sleeve. Faint acrid smell mixed with the industrial detergent.
- **The Bottle:** Empty bottle of expensive Russian vodka on the concrete floor near the basket. Label still visible.
- **The Room:** Smells like industrial detergent and something chemical. Something wrong. The smell makes people's eyes water.
- **The Phone:** In Richie's pocket. Screen cracked. Battery dead. Time of last activity: 10:47 PM.
- **The Staging:** Laundry baskets stacked around. Bottles of industrial cleaner on shelves. A card reader for the time clock. Door propped slightly open from inside.

**HOST:** "Let players see the body. Let them react. Irina is crying, speaking Russian. Marco looks frozen. Val is pale."

---

### THE AFTERMATH (0:40–0:45)
### **One of Us Did This**

**HOST:** "Everyone gathers in the hotel lobby. Rosa Martinez is on her phone with the police, speaking calmly. She hangs up:

"'Police on their way. Due to an incident on the main island, they won't arrive for approximately two hours. Nobody leaves the hotel. Nobody touches anything in the laundry area. I've locked the service entrance. We wait. We figure this out quietly, or we let the police figure it out. Either way, nobody leaves.'

"Rosa's competence is terrifying. She's managed worse than this."

**HOST NOTE:** Scan for reactions:
- **Val:** Anger flashing across her face, then fear. She grips her drink tightly.
- **Pam:** Looks physically ill. She was just saying she needed to "talk to Richie" and now he's dead.
- **Irina:** Shaking. Retreating. Won't make eye contact.
- **Sunny:** Controlled. Almost too controlled. Like she's managing a situation.
- **Marco:** Looking at Gloria. Gloria giving Marco a look that says very clearly: "Say nothing."

**HOST:** "Someone says what everyone is thinking:

"'One of us did this.'

"Silence.

"'One of us poisoned Richard Rathbone. And now we're all stuck here for two hours with a dead man and no police. That narrows down the suspects, doesn't it?'"

**HOST:** "End of Round 1. Take a break. Get water. Breathe.

"When we come back, everyone's a suspect. And everyone has something to lose."
