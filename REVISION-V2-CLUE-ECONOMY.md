# Dirty Laundry v3.1 — Clue Economy Revision (Proposal)

**Status:** PROPOSAL — for Shane's review
**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Constraint:** No dossier changes. Only `00-Master-Storyline.md`, the four round cards, the host guide, and the props list change.

---

## Why this revision exists

Three problems in the current draft (already noted in `Story Consistency.md`):

1. **The killer's case is the weakest physical case in the game.** Only one prop (the insulin pen) uniquely points to Gloria. Misdirect suspects (Val, Marco, Irina) each have more physical evidence against them than the actual killer.
2. **Revelations are unblocked acting prompts.** Cards say "the secret surfaces" without specifying who holds the prop, who speaks the line, who reacts, or what cues the host calls.
3. **Clue distribution is upside-down.** Eight suspects each have 1–2 clues + body-language tells. The board is too crowded; players can't reduce.

This revision keeps Gloria as the killer, keeps Stephen's two-method fix, keeps every dossier identical. It changes **what the host shows and who says what** so the case lands cleanly without depending on every player nailing a body-language tell.

---

## Design principles for the new clue economy

1. **Every revelation = prop + person + line + reaction.** No "the topic comes up." The Mentalist calls a cue. A named character produces a named prop. They speak a scripted line. One or two named characters have a scripted reaction.
2. **Physical clues must be visually loud.** A dark brown coffee-stain on a collar, not "yellowish residue." A sweating-through-the-shirt handkerchief, not "looks nervous." The poison reads as "spilled coffee" at first glance — that's the camouflage. The chain locks when the audience sees the same brown in four places.
3. **Body-language tells must be operatic.** "Pam can't stand up straight, hangs off Gloria's shoulder" — not "Pam looks fuzzy." The audience should be able to laugh and recognize the tell at the same time.
4. **The killer carries four physical clues.** All visible to the audience. All can be produced on demand if a player asks.
5. **The misdirect is three suspects, not eight.** Val, Marco, Irina. Each gets three obvious physical clues. Other suspects get one over-the-top tell that resolves them early.
6. **Eliminations happen on-camera.** Pam is cleared in Round 2 (the bleach + scent bottle scene). Sunny's federal reveal moves to Round 2. Doug's "Pittsburgh receipt" is revealed as an obvious fake in Round 2. Zane is cleared in Round 2 via Val's alibi holding. Dex is cleared in Round 2 via Jimmy's coffee testimony. By the start of Round 3, the live board is **Val, Marco, Irina, Gloria.**

## 2) HOW TO STAGE THE NECK STAB WOUND

The body proxy is stuffed clothes in a laundry basket, face-down. The neck needs to read clearly when players walk up to the basket and look in.

**Staging recipe (cheap, obvious, reusable):**

1. **The neck.** Use a flesh-toned pillowcase or beige tube sock as the "neck" stuffing visible above the shirt collar. Tuck under the shirt so only ~3 inches of "skin" shows.
2. **The puncture mark.** Cut a small red felt circle (size of a dime). Glue it to the side of the neck. In the center, glue a single black bead (the "needle hole"). Around the bead, draw a thin red ring with red Sharpie to suggest a small bruise halo.
3. **Optional upgrade ($8):** Buy a single Halloween "stab wound" prosthetic from Amazon ("fake injection mark sticker," "needle puncture decal"). Stick it on the neck stuffing.
4. **Drip line.** A 2-inch streak of thick red Kool-Aid running from the puncture down to the collar — and right next to it, a parallel **brown drip** (brown Sharpie or coffee) running from the puncture down into the brown collar stain. Two drips, two colors, side by side. The audience instantly reads "she was poisoned AND injected" without needing a chemical analysis report.
5. **Corroborating props in the corridor (planted before guests arrive):**
   - **Kool-Aid-stained cotton ball** on the floor 3 feet from the basket. White cotton, single drop of thick red Kool-Aid in the center.
   - **Tiny clear plastic needle cap** (you can pull this off a real insulin pen, or buy a sewing-needle cap) on the floor near the laundry door.
   - These are NOT pointed out by the host. The host lets a sharp-eyed player find one. If nobody notices, Pepe finds the cotton ball during R3 Beat 6.

**Why this works:** The audience sees a dark brown stain on the shirt (poison, disguised as a coffee spill) AND a clearly visible red wound on the neck (injection). Two methods, two visual signatures, no chemistry required.
