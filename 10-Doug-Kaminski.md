## CHARACTER 7: DOUGLAS "THE DUKE" KAMINSKI | The Porta Potty Proprietor

**Tagline:** *Pittsburgh charm meets Caribbean laundromat. All his jokes are about shit—literal and figurative.*

**Era:** Early fifties with grandfatherly energy. Big man, big laugh, big presence. Fills every room and most doorframes.

**Public Persona:** Boisterous Pittsburgh businessman. Porta potty rental franchise empire (Kaminski's Portable Sanitation—KPS). Sense of humor about the business. Makes porta potty jokes CONSTANTLY: "You know what they say about the porta potty business? It's a crappy job but somebody's gotta do it." Never tires of this. Absolutely massive in height, volume, presence. "Talks for a living, even if it's all shit."

**Real Story:** Porta potty business is perfect money laundering front. Cash-heavy, construction sites, fudgeable municipal contracts, zero oversight. Been cleaning dirty money for five years—initially his own tax evasion, increasingly for Gloria's cartel. Financial dependency runs deep: Gloria needs his cash business, he needs her volume to justify expansion.

Always, ALWAYS late to everything. Running gag bordering on pathological. Late to own wedding. Late to father's funeral. "Sorry, had a situation with a unit in Scranton." Nobody questions it because he's The Duke—lateness is his brand. This alibi is perfect because he's always late.

Soft spot: Val Montecito. Obvious, slightly sad crush. Tries to impress with business deals. Brings her Primanti Brothers sandwiches and Steelers merchandise from Pittsburgh. She finds him amusing and useful. He'd protect her from anything—doesn't know about her trafficking operation.

**Connection to Richie:** Porta potty contract with Val's hotel—Richie would testify it's a money laundering front. Doug's entire operation at risk. His lateness night of murder is suspicious—supposed to arrive at 8 PM, didn't show until midnight. Four hours unaccounted.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Val Montecito**: Crush. Business partner. Brings her sandwiches and merchandise. She finds him charming in harmless-uncle way. He'd take a bullet for her.
- **Gloria Reyes**: Financial codependency. She launders through his business. Mild flirtation masks serious economic entanglement.
- **Dex Calloway**: Old friends from Philadelphia bar scene. Friendly rivals. Owes Dex $15K from poker. "I'll get you next week, Dex. Swear on my mother's porta potty."
- **Pam Worthington**: Mildly flirting as backup plan. She's not interested but appreciates the attention.

**Financial Entanglements:**
- Launders $40-60K monthly for Gloria through KPS
- Owes Dex $15K from poker debt
- Millions in operation jeopardy from Richie's testimony
- Contract with Val is real AND a front for money movement
- Expansion plans contingent on Gloria's volume

**Alibi:**
- **Plausible:** Porta potty emergency in Pittsburgh. Phone records and texts confirm unit overflow at construction site. Dispatch confirms service call. History of lateness provides context. Tracks perfectly with 8 PM supposed arrival, midnight actual arrival.
- **Implausible:** Phone calls can be scheduled in advance. Texts pre-written. Someone else could handle Pittsburgh while he handled something else. Four unaccounted hours is significant. His brand of lateness could cover actual activity. Technology can deceive.

**Motive: MODERATE** (6/10)
- Business at risk from Richie's testimony
- Least personally threatened compared to others
- Could theoretically flee to another jurisdiction
- But protecting empire, Gloria relationship, and Val—that's enough if cornered
- Problem: his lateness is documented alibi, not weakness

**Red Herrings & Layers:**
- Appears least sophisticated (untrue—laundering operation is complex)
- Humor and size mask operational competence
- Crush on Val is genuine but makes him protective
- Friendship with Dex is real but poker debt is leverage
- Lateness is alibi but also suspicious
- Gloria relationship is both protector and threat

**Secrets:**
- Porta potty empire is money laundering front
- Launders $40-60K monthly for Gloria's cartel
- Crush on Val borders on obsessive (protective instincts)
- Knows Gloria is cartel, cooperates willingly
- Owes Dex significant poker debt
- Four-hour gap on murder night (late arrival, early departure?)
