## CHARACTER 5: GLORIA "GLO" REYES | The Cartel Mom

**Tagline:** *Soccer mom by day. Cartel operator by necessity. Vengeance patient but never forgotten.*

**Era:** Late forties, looks early forties. Botox isn't her secret—genetics and compartmentalization are.

**Public Persona:** Looks like a mom. Is a mom. Nice linen, excellent blow-out, Range Rover, soccer-mom energy. Chairs the Gasparilla Children's Parade committee. On PTA at Academy of the Holy Names. Best deviled eggs at every potluck. Here as a "consultant" for a shell company.

**Real Story:** Got into drug dealing almost by accident. Suburban Tampa mom who needed money and schedule flexibility. Started small, turned out to be very good. Now runs serious mid-level operation across Florida and Caribbean. Mother of three: Marco (early twenties, the valet), Isabella (college sophomore, friends with Val), and Diego (deceased—killed two years ago in cartel ambush triggered by bad intel Richie provided). 

Boundaries: no violence against families, no drugs near schools. But cross her and she's ruthless. Bakes cookies for sorority fundraisers and orders hits on informants—sometimes same Tuesday.

**Connection to Richie:** Richie owed Gloria $80K for cocaine. But the real wound: Richie's bad intelligence got Diego killed. He gave rival info about a Reyes shipment. Diego was guarding it. Gloria has never forgiven but stayed patient. Richie's testimony would expose Marco, Irina, entire operation.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Marco** (son): Loyal but terrified for safety. Doesn't know he's skimming $5K/month or planning to flee.
- **Irina Volkov**: Her handler/operator. Pays for Irina's mother's cancer treatment ($4K/month) and daughter Katya's school tuition. This is leverage, not generosity.
- **Dex Calloway**: Former employee. Knows his real identity, crimes, everything. Has leverage through Irina.
- **Pam Worthington**: GENUINE best friend from Tampa charity gala three years ago. Covers for Pam's blackouts. Most authentic friendship in Gloria's life.
- **Val Montecito**: Gloria's daughter Isabella is friends with Val. Gloria knows about Val's trafficking. Val has no idea Gloria is cartel.
- **Doug Kaminski**: Launders money through porta potty contracts. Mild flirtation—finds his humor charming.
- **Zane Nakamura**: Owes her $200K, chronically behind on payments.

**Financial Entanglements:**
- Owed $80K by Richie (unpaid)
- Owed $200K by Zane (slow payment)
- Launders through Doug's business ($40-60K monthly)
- Pays $4K/month for Irina's mother's treatment
- Pays for Irina's daughter's school tuition (~$2K/month)
- Unaware Marco is skimming $5K/month

**Alibi:**
- **Plausible:** Conference room with two attendees. Visibly engaged, opposite side of hotel from laundry. Two independent witnesses, though both owe her favors.
- **Implausible:** Side exit to courtyard, three-minute walk to service area. Doesn't need to do own dirty work. Orchestration through Irina is her style.

**Motive: EXTREME** (9/10)
- Richie got her son killed
- Owes her significant money
- Testimony destroys operation and imprisons Marco
- Two years of simmering vengeance
- Problem: she's too smart to be obvious

**Red Herrings & Layers:**
- Appears least threatened (untrue—most threatened)
- Friendship with Pam is genuine but gives her cover
- Business consulting cover is legitimate-looking but thin
- Isabella's friendship with Val complicates everything
- Marco's skimming creates additional motive she doesn't know about

**Secrets:**
- Runs multi-state cartel operation
- Marco is her son (identity unknown to most)
- Richie's intelligence got Diego killed
- Leverage over Irina and Dex through family
- Launders through Doug
- Completely unaware of Marco's embezzlement
