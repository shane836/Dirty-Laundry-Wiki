# Val Montecito — The Hotel Owner

**Female | Early 30s | SUSPECT**

---

## Who You Are

You own Heathens Hollow, this boutique hotel. Stunning, charismatic, always holding a champagne glass. Your wealthy father bankrolled the hotel as your "redemption arc" after some messy years in New York. You look like you were born poolside. Underneath the glamour, you're holding this place together with duct tape and crime.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **You run a trafficking and escort operation through the hotel** to keep it financially afloat. It's disguised as a legitimate hospitality business. Without it, the hotel goes under.
2. **Paloma was blackmailing you.** She knew about the operation and was demanding hush money to stay quiet. You'd been paying, but she kept raising the price.
3. **Your father threatened to cut you off** if the hotel doesn't turn a profit. The operation is the only thing keeping you solvent — and keeping your family from finding out how bad things really are.

## Your Alibi

You were in your private suite with Zane from about 9:15 PM onward. You say you were "relaxing." In reality, you and Zane were in crisis mode — his crypto wallet got destroyed and a fortune tied to your operation vanished with it. You didn't kill Paloma, but you can't explain what you were actually doing without exposing everything.

## Your Connections

- **Paloma** — Had an affair with her. She dumped you when she decided to flip. Then she started blackmailing you.
- **Zane** — Business partner and crypto manager. He's in love with you. You see him as useful.
- **Marco** — Your employee. Charming. You flirt, but it's surface-level.
- **Gloria** — You know her as a family friend. You don't know about the cartel.

## Your Props

Champagne glass, oversized sunglasses, hotel owner's override key card (labeled)
