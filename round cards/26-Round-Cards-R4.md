# Round 4 Cards — "The Reveal"
### In-Game Time: Midnight | Flipboard: ROUND 4 — MIDNIGHT

**Scene:** The vote. The confession. Big reactions. This round is short and theatrical — 15 minutes max. **Gloria carries the weight.** Everyone else just gets to react big.

---

## THE VOTE

The Mentalist addresses the room:

*"You've heard the evidence. You've heard the alibis. You've seen the cracks. It's time. Who killed Paloma Rathbone?"*

Players vote — verbal, written ballot, or show of hands. The Mentalist tallies.

**If players vote Gloria:** Gloria stands and confesses. Run the script below.

**If players DON'T vote Gloria** (they get it wrong, or split the vote, or chicken out): the Mentalist takes the floor. With dramatic flourish:

*"Let me read you the room. Mrs. Reyes? Stand up, please."*

*"Five browns on this table. A burnt note in Mrs. Volkov's apron. A Sharpie disguised as an insulin pen. A son she buried two years ago. A son she's protecting tonight. A watch checked four times in ten minutes before she stepped out for what was definitely not a restroom break."*

*"Ladies and gentlemen — you missed it. The killer is Gloria Reyes."*

Gloria stands. Run the confession.

---

## GLORIA — THE CONFESSION

**This is the only scripted speech in the round.** Gloria delivers it from memory, paraphrased, or read from her sealed envelope. Take your time. Look at Marco.

> "Yes. I killed her.
>
> The shirt was always the plan. Remote. Clean. I gave Irina the compound. She delivered it through the laundry. Paloma would get sick slowly — by morning it would look like a medical event. That was the professional plan.
>
> But at 10:25, I saw her through the conference room door. Stumbling. Grabbing the wall. Still alive. The shirt should have been enough.
>
> But I couldn't sit in that room and wait.
>
> I found her near the laundry. She recognized me. *'Gloria? What are you—'*
>
> I injected the paralytic. Ninety seconds.
>
> The shirt was the operation. The injection was me. A mother.
>
> My son Diego was twenty-two. Paloma gave one piece of bad information to the wrong people and called it cost of doing business. She never lost a night of sleep. I've lost every night for two years.
>
> She was going to testify. She was going to bring down everyone in this room — including Marco, who has done nothing except be born into the wrong family.
>
> A mother protects her children. Even the dead ones."

*Looks at Marco.* "I'm sorry you had to see this."

---

## EVERYONE ELSE — REACTION DIRECTIONS

You don't have lines. You have **big, theatrical reactions.** Lean in. Soap opera. Telenovela. The bigger the better.

If a line below feels right, say it. Otherwise just react big and stay in character.

### MARCO REYES
- **Devastated.** You knew. On some level you knew. Hearing it is different.
- One word, if anything: **"Mom."**
- Or just don't speak. Sit down. Hold your head.

### VAL MONTECITO
- **Shock.** Then anger. Then a thousand-yard stare.
- *"She sat next to me. She comforted Pam. She orchestrated everything."*
- Drop your champagne glass for real this time, if it isn't already broken.

### ZANE NAKAMURA
- **Pure relief** that it wasn't Val. Then horror.
- *"Oh my god. Oh my god. Oh my god."* (Just keep saying it.)
- Hold Val's hand. You're still in financial ruin, but you're not a murder suspect.

### DEX CALLOWAY
- Look at Irina across the room. **Long. Quiet. Devastating.**
- Pour yourself a drink. Slide the thermos across the bar. Don't speak.

### PAM WORTHINGTON
- **Operatic grief.** Wail. Drink directly from the fishbowl. Slosh wine on yourself.
- *"My BEST FRIEND is a MURDERER."* (Loud. Slurred. Repeat as needed.)
- Try to hug Gloria. Get blocked by Sunny.

### DOUG KAMINSKI
- **The jokes are over.** Stunned silence.
- One line: *"Aw, hell, Mollie."*
- Sit down. Look at the karaoke machine. Don't sing.

### IRINA VOLKOV
- **Quiet.** You knew. You're just relieved everyone else knows now.
- Look at Dex. He looks back. That's the goodbye.

### SUNNY PATEL
- **Federal-agent calm.** This is your case now.
- Step toward Gloria. Don't grab her — she's not running. *"Mrs. Reyes. You'll come with me."*

### LORRAINE CHU
- **Vindicated.** You called it all night.
- *"I told you. I told you she was too composed."*

### GERALD FINCH
- **Wrecked.** You covered for her. That's going to haunt you.
- Stare at the table. Don't speak.

### ROSA MARTINEZ
- **Professional.** Radio out: *"This is Rosa. Police are pulling up. Send them to the lobby."*

### SASHA KIMURA
- **Quiet vindication.** You held the envelope for two days. *"She knew. I told you — she knew."*
- Cry, finally.

### DIEGO REYES (Ghost)
- Stand behind Gloria during the confession. **Don't move. Don't speak.** Your presence is the punctuation.
- When Gloria finishes, step closer to her. Just for a beat. Then drift away.

### ZOYA DAVENPORT
- **Slow nod.** *"I hoped I was wrong. Thirty years."*

### REV. JIMMY WHITFIELD
- **Make the sign of the cross.** *"Lord have mercy."*
- Offer Gloria your hand. She doesn't take it. That's okay.

### FIONA BEAUMONT
- **Take the photo.** Of Gloria. Of Marco. Of Diego. *"This is the shot."*

### DESTINY RIVERS
- **Camera up.** *"This is going viral. This is GOING VIRAL."*
- Then put the camera down. Even you know when to stop.

### NADIA OSEI
- **Cue the music.** Slow, mournful steel-drum cover of something inappropriate. (Pre-load it.)

### PEPE STEVENS
- **Hands in pockets.** *"Tell me where to send the body, Rosa."*

## WRAP-UP

The Mentalist closes:

*"Paloma Rathbone was a lot of things — charming, manipulative, brilliant, cruel. She made enemies the way some people make friends: effortlessly and in every direction. But she didn't deserve to die in a laundry basket.*

*Gloria Reyes is a mother who buried her son and decided someone had to answer for it. Whether that makes her a villain or something more complicated — that's not for me to decide.*

*Police are here. The investigation is over. But the questions aren't. They never are.*

*Thank you for playing Dirty Laundry."*

**Game over. Applause. Drinks. Discuss.**
