# Round 3 Cards — "The Investigation"
### In-Game Time: 11:00 PM – Midnight | Flipboard: ROUND 3 — 11:00 PM

**Scene:** The longest round. Hard evidence. The live board entering R3 is **Val, Marco, Irina, Gloria**. Same conversational format as R2 — players talk to each other, characters share clues and reveals when the right person asks. The Mentalist drives **two big visual beats** that need the whole room: the five matching browns, and Gloria cornered.

---

## How This Round Works (read this first)

R3 is investigation by conversation. Each character below has **clues to share** and **people to seek out**. The Mentalist sets the tone — *"You've heard the alibis. Now press them. Look at the body. Open bags. Ask the questions you wouldn't ask earlier."* — and then circulates while players go to work.

Two **room-stopping Mentalist beats** anchor the round (the five browns + Gloria cornered). Everything else lands in pairs, threes, and small clusters. Pace ~45 minutes. Keep the misdirection breathing — let players commit to "It's Val!" before Marco looks guilty, then let them commit to "It's Marco!" before the Diego reveal turns it.

## MENTALIST BEATS (the two room-stopping moments)

The conversation does most of the work — Val looks guilty first (override card + Zane's break + Nadia's silhouette), then Marco looks guilty (master key + valet gap + dropped keys), then Zoya names Marco as Gloria's son and Diego speaks. **Hold for theorizing between each turn of the screw.** Players need 3–4 minutes to commit to a theory before the next turn dismantles it.

When the energy peaks, the Mentalist runs the two big beats:

### BEAT A — The Five Browns (the visual climax)
The chain locks here. Run this when Irina has broken, Pepe has produced the coffee tin, the cotton ball, and the needle cap, and the room is ready to look at the body.

- **MENTALIST:** "Mrs. Reyes. Your bag."
- **GLORIA:** "You can't be serious."
- **MENTALIST:** "I am Fortunado the Magnificent and I am perfectly serious. Empty it."
- **SUNNY:** Step in, badge in hand. Demand it.
- **GLORIA:** Place items on the table one at a time:
  1. The printed Diego photo.
  2. The handkerchief — red Kool-Aid drop + brown smear visible.
  3. The brown Sharpie wrapped in masking tape labeled "INSULIN."
- **MENTALIST:** *(picks up the Sharpie, peels back the tape)* "Mrs. Reyes, you are not diabetic. And this is a Sharpie."
- **SUNNY:** Hold the Sharpie next to the collar next to the handkerchief next to Gloria's stained cuff next to the powder in Irina's coffee tin. **Five matching browns in a line.**
- **MENTALIST:** Walk to Dex's thermos at the bar. "Mr. Calloway, the thermos."
- **DEX:** Pull it out. Open it. Steam, real coffee, different shade.
- **MENTALIST:** "*That* is coffee. *This* is the same brown as the stain on Ms. Rathbone's collar."
- **GLORIA:** Says nothing.

### BEAT B — Val cleared, Marco the eyewitness
The Mentalist closes the round. Pepe has the work order. The room turns.

- **PEPE:** Hand over the maintenance work order signed by Val at 10:05. "Floor 3. Leaking unit. The override card was a maintenance call."
- **MENTALIST:** "Val Montecito is cleared." **(Val exhales for the first time all round.)**
- **MENTALIST:** "Mr. Reyes. What did you see?"
- **MARCO:** Drop your keys. Pick them up. Look at Gloria. *"I saw her near the service stairwell. 10:25. She had her hand in her tote. I knew. I've known all night."*

**End of Round 3.** Hand out Round 4 cards. Give Gloria her confession envelope if you haven't already.
