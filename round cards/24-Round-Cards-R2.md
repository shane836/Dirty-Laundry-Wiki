# Round 2 Cards — "Everyone's a Suspect"
### In-Game Time: 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM | Flipboard: ROUND 2 — 10:00 PM

**Scene:** Everyone's inside (main structure). The party is over. The Mentalist sets the room, then **turns the players loose to talk to each other.** This is not a round-robin — it's a cocktail-party investigation. Players move, pair off, and trade alibis and secrets in conversation.

---

## How This Round Works (read this first)

The Mentalist opens, then says: *"Talk to each other. Find your connections. Get your story straight — or pick someone else's apart. I'm watching."*

Each character below gets:
- **Your alibi** — the story you tell when asked. Lead with this.
- **Share in conversation** — secrets/observations you should drop when the right person asks (or when you sense an opening). These were the old "revelations."
- **Seek out** — named characters you should actively go find this round. Connections matter — start with them.
- **Yes-and rule** — if someone asks you something not on your card, play along. Make it up consistently with your character. Don't break the fiction.

The Mentalist drops **three room-stopping reveals** during the round (see "Mentalist beats" at the end). Otherwise, players drive the conversation. Pace: ~30 minutes total. Mentalist circulates, eavesdrops, prompts shy players, and pulls the trigger on the three big beats when the energy is right.

## MENTALIST BEATS (the three room-stopping moments)

The conversation does most of the work. **Three times** during the round, the Mentalist takes the floor and runs a scripted beat. These are the load-bearing reveals that can't be left to chance.

Drop them roughly **10, 20, and 28 minutes** into the round (or whenever the energy peaks/dips and needs a punch).

### BEAT A — The Pam clear (mid-round)
The Mentalist calls the room to attention.

- **MENTALIST:** "Mr. Stevens, you said something about your closet."
- **PEPE:** Holds up the broken spray bottle and a small fragrance bottle that matches Pam's vial. "Mrs. Worthington broke into my supply closet around 10. Knocked this spray bottle off the shelf — that's her burn. Grabbed this fragrance bottle on the way out. It's room scent. We have forty."
- **PAM:** "I did?"
- **MENTALIST:** "Mrs. Worthington, you couldn't find the laundry with a map. You're cleared."
- **PAM:** Toasts the room with the fishbowl. Sloshes wine.

### BEAT B — Sunny stands up (federal stakes)
The Mentalist has been counting.

- **MENTALIST:** "Mr. Patel. You've been very quiet. Stand up."
- **SUNNY:** Stands. Drops the corporate persona. Opens the padfolio. Produces the federal badge and the printed indictment list.
  - "I'm not a consultant. IRS-Interpol joint task force. Paloma was my informant. She was going to testify against everyone in this room on Monday. Whoever killed her destroyed three years of work and saved every operation in this hotel."
- **VAL:** Drop your champagne glass. (Shatters or doesn't — actor's choice.)
- **ZANE:** "Oh god."
- **GLORIA:** Do not move.
- **DOUG:** "Aw, hell."
- **MENTALIST:** Hand the indictment list to the room. Players read names. Existential motive sharpens.
- **SASHA:** This is your moment to step forward with the envelope. "Then I think you should have this." Hand the sealed envelope to Sunny.

### BEAT C — The watch count (Gloria flagged for the first time)
Late in the round. Gloria's been checking her watch and Lorraine has been counting.

- **MENTALIST:** *(checks pocket watch, looks at Gloria)* "Mrs. Reyes. That's four times in the last ten minutes you've checked your watch. Are we keeping you?"
- **GLORIA:** Smiles. "Force of habit. PTA."
- **GERALD:** Visibly sweat. Mop your face with your now-soaked handkerchief.
- **LORRAINE:** Quietly, evenly: "Gerald checked his watch four times too. He was watching hers."

**End of Round 2.** The live board going into Round 3: **Val, Marco, Irina, Gloria.** Pam, Doug, Zane, Dex, and Sunny are cleared as the killer (though Doug, Zane, and Dex are exposed for other things). Hand out Round 3 cards. Break.
