# Round 2 Cards — "Everyone's a Suspect"
### In-Game Time: 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM | Flipboard: ROUND 2 — 10:00 PM

**Scene:** Everyone's inside (main structure). The party is over. The Mentalist runs the alibi round, then secrets start surfacing.

---

## MAJOR CHARACTERS

### VAL MONTECITO
- **Your alibi:** "I was in my suite from about 9:15. Zane was with me. We were... relaxing." Hesitate on "relaxing."
- If pressed on when you last saw Paloma, be vague. "This morning? Maybe last night?" You're lying about something — but maybe not about murder.
- If the beach photos come up (Fiona or Mentalist brings them), get defensive. "Where are those photos? Delete them."
- When the master key / override card comes up, stay quiet. You own the only override card. Don't volunteer that.

### MARCO REYES
- **Your alibi:** "Valet stand. The whole time. I've got the log sheets." Be defensive. Show the log.
- You took a 15-minute break around 10 PM. If pressed: "Bathroom." But the bathroom is right by the valet stand and you were gone too long.
- When Paloma's name comes up, your jaw tightens. If someone pushes: "She called me a 'local punk' over a parking scratch. A SCRATCH."
- Do NOT reveal Gloria is your mother. Don't even look at her when she's speaking.

### ZANE NAKAMURA
- **Your alibi:** "What Val said. Her suite. The whole time." Match Val's story too perfectly. Nod too eagerly.
- If asked what you were "working on," exchange a look with Val. Don't answer clearly.
- When the margarita incident comes up, get bitter: "Paloma spilled margaritas on me twice. Soaked my hardware wallet. I started thinking it wasn't clumsiness."
- When the override card is mentioned, look at Val nervously.

### DEX CALLOWAY
- **Your alibi:** "Behind the bar. Ask anyone. I took a standard 20-minute break around 10:15." Casual, confident.
- If pushed on where you went during the break: "Gotta hydrate, man." Deflect with humor. Do NOT say you were bringing Irina food.
- When someone asks if you and Doug know each other, pause. Something passes between you. "We've... crossed paths."
- If asked about the margarita spill on Paloma: smile. Don't answer. That IS the answer.

### GLORIA REYES
- **Your alibi:** "Conference room. Tax shelter strategies with Gerald Finch and Lorraine Chu." Smooth, prepared. Gerald and Lorraine nod before you finish speaking.
- Be calm. Steady hands. Steady voice. Check your watch once — catch yourself doing it.
- When Pam gets emotional, put your arm around her. "Leave her alone. She's grieving."
- When the master key swipe is announced, show no reaction. You're the calmest person in the room. That should worry people.

### PAM WORTHINGTON
- **Your alibi:** "I was... at the party. I think. I had several drinks. I'm honestly fuzzy after 9:30." You look like you're about to cry.
- You have a chemical burn on your hand. You don't know how you got it. If someone notices: panic.
- If the "we're done" text comes up or someone asks about your relationship with Paloma: cry. Won't answer. It was complicated.
- Let Gloria comfort you. She's your best friend and you need her right now.

### DOUG KAMINSKI
- **Your alibi:** "I wasn't even HERE until midnight! Porta potty emergency in Pittsburgh!" Pull out your phone, show texts and call logs. Be defensive immediately.
- When Rosa mentions you came through the service entrance, go pale. "I... it was closer to the parking lot."
- If Dex is asked whether you two know each other, give him a look. You've got history but neither of you planned to admit it.
- Crack porta potty jokes to deflect tension. Nobody's laughing.

### IRINA VOLKOV
- **Your alibi:** "[Halting English] I am working. Laundry. All night. I find her. I am not... I did not..." Shake. Be nearly incoherent.
- When the master key swipe at 10:07 is announced, don't answer. You have a master key. Let that silence hang.
- If anyone asks about the garment bag delivery at 7 PM, look terrified. "Is normal. Garment service. For guest."
- Do NOT break character with your English yet. Save that for Round 3.

### SUNNY PATEL
- **Your alibi:** "At the party the whole time. I spoke with Val, then Marco, then Dex, then Pam." List precisely. Too precisely.
- If anyone asks what you talked about with each person: hesitate for the first time. That hesitation is everything.
- If Zane looks at you suspiciously, hold eye contact. Don't flinch.
- Do NOT reveal you're a federal agent yet. That's Round 3.
