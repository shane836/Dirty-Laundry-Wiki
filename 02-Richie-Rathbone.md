# Paloma Rathbone — The Victim

**The Tax Influencer | Female | Middle-aged**
**Played by: Dina (who will be cooking — lean into the comedy)**

---

## Your Situation

You are dead. You were found face-down in a laundry basket in the hotel laundry room. You don't get to defend yourself. But people will talk about you all night — and not all of it will be flattering. Enjoy being the most discussed woman at the party.

You're the host's cooking partner tonight, so you'll be in and out of the kitchen. When you're around other guests, you can:
- React to things people say about you (dramatically, from beyond the grave)
- Drop cryptic hints if asked directly ("I knew too much. That was always my problem.")
- Play up the comedy of being dead at your own party
- Deny nothing and confirm nothing

---

## Who You Were

Russian-born. Charming, manipulative, brilliant — and a handsy drunk. You built a career in offshore tax law through sheer force of personality — the woman you called when you wanted to move money somewhere the government couldn't follow. So good you became a "tax influencer." Organized this entire conference as your networking empire.

You drank expensive Russian vodka — a lot of it. After a few drinks, you got touchy, grabby, too close. You used intimacy as currency and alcohol as an excuse. You had an affair with Val Montecito (the hotel owner) and a complicated thing with Pam Worthington. Your estranged spouse is back in Russia and hasn't spoken to you in months.

## Why You're Dead

Two weeks ago, you decided you were done helping bad people do worse things. You made a deal with Sunny Patel — who you believed was a management consultant but who is actually a federal agent. You'd provide evidence against everyone at the conference, testify, and disappear into witness protection.

But you also created enemies along the way:
- You gave bad intelligence to a rival cartel that got Gloria's son Diego killed two years ago
- You were blackmailing Val to keep her trafficking operation quiet
- You deliberately destroyed Zane's crypto wallet
- You owed Gloria money for product
- You humiliated Marco publicly

The conference was supposed to be your last hurrah. Instead, it became your tomb.

---

## Body Proxy Setup

Stuff clothes with pillows/towels to create a body shape. Place face-down in a laundry basket. Add:
- An empty Russian vodka bottle nearby
- A dress shirt with yellowish powder on the collar (flour or cornstarch works)
- A cracked old phone

Place in the side structure (laundry room / crime scene area) before the party starts. Cover with a sheet until the "body discovery" moment in Round 1.
