## Crime Scene Props (set up before party in side structure)

| Prop | How to Make It |
|---|---|
| **Body proxy** | Stuff clothes with pillows/towels. Arrange face-down in a laundry basket. Dress in a light-colored button-down shirt (white or pastel — brown stains must read clearly). Use flesh-toned pillowcase or beige tube sock for ~3 inches of "neck" stuffing visible above the collar. |
| **Poisoned shirt stain** | **Dark brown wet stain on the collar** — apply with strong brewed coffee or brown Sharpie scribble. Reads as a "coffee spill" at first glance. That's the camouflage. Visible from 10 feet. |
| **Neck puncture wound** | **Glue a dime-sized red felt circle to the side of the neck stuffing. In the center, glue a single black bead (the needle hole). Around the bead, draw a thin red ring with red Sharpie for a bruise halo.** Optional upgrade: $8 Halloween "stab wound" / "needle puncture" decal from Amazon. |
| **Dual drip lines** | From the puncture, run **two parallel drips** down to the collar: one red (red Kool-Aid mixed thick), one brown (brown Sharpie or coffee). Side by side. The audience reads "stabbed AND poisoned" instantly. |
| **Kool-Aid-stained cotton ball** | Plant on corridor floor ~3 ft from basket before guests arrive. White cotton, single drop of red Kool-Aid in center. |
| **Needle cap (labeled "INSULIN")** | Plant on corridor floor ~10 ft down the hall. Small clear plastic cap with masking tape and "INSULIN" written across it in Sharpie — matches Gloria's pen exactly. |
| **Empty vodka bottle** | Any glass bottle with a fancy label. Bonus if you print a Russian vodka label. |
| **Cracked phone** | Old phone with cracked screen, or tape a phone screen protector that's cracked |
| **Crime scene tape** | Yellow caution tape or string. Cordon off the laundry area after body discovery. |

## Color discipline (CRITICAL)

The case is built on **five matching browns**: the collar stain, the powder in Irina's "Café Cubano" tin, the brown Sharpie inside Gloria's tote (her "insulin pen"), the smear on Gloria's handkerchief, and Gloria's cuff stain. They all come from the same brown Sharpie. They must all read as the same brown.

**No other brown garments in the room.** When you brief guests on costumes, tell them: Caribbean pastels — pinks, blues, whites, yellows, greens. **No brown clothing.** The only allowed brown item is Dex's coffee thermos (intentional misdirect — actual hot coffee, brown like the poison). Everything else in brown belongs to the killer's chain.

When the Mentalist lines the five browns up on the table in R3 Beat 7, the visual climax only works if brown is rare in the room.
