# Costume Guide — Dirty Laundry v3

Send this to players ahead of the party so they can dress the part. No plot details included.

**Setting:** A boutique hotel in the Grand Caymans during the after-party of an exclusive "Tax Optimization Seminar." Think tropical noir — resort wear with an edge.

---

## Major Characters

### Val Montecito — The Hotel Owner
**Female | Early 30s**
Silk or satin slip dress (tropical print or jewel tone). Gold jewelry — layered necklaces, bangles. Strappy heels or embellished sandals. Oversized sunglasses pushed up on head. Champagne glass as permanent accessory. Beachy waves hair.
**Vibe:** A luxury resort ad that started ordering people around.

### Marco Reyes — The Valet
**Male | Early 20s**
Hotel uniform: black or white button-down, sleeves rolled. Dark slacks, polished shoes. Valet name tag. Car keys on lanyard clipped to belt.
**Vibe:** Too pretty to be "just the help."

### Zane Nakamura — The Crypto Hustler
**Male | Late 20s**
Designer hoodie or graphic tee under a blazer. Slim joggers or tailored shorts. Flashy sneakers. Oversized watch. Phone in hand at all times. AirPods in one ear.
**Vibe:** Closing a deal poolside while ordering a $40 smoothie.

### Dex Calloway — The Bartender
**Male | Mid 30s**
Clean white button-down or henley, sleeves rolled. Dark apron waist-tied. Dark jeans. Bar towel over shoulder. Simple leather watch.
**Vibe:** Clearly has a past but won't tell you unless you order neat bourbon.

### Gloria "Glo" Reyes — The Consultant
**Female | Late 40s**
Crisp linen blouse or wrap top. Tailored resort pants or midi skirt. Wedge sandals or nice flats. Pearl or gold stud earrings. Designer tote. Reading glasses on a chain.
**Vibe:** On every board and committee at the country club, simultaneously.

### Pam Worthington — The Divorcee
**Female | Mid 40s**
Bold resort dress or jumpsuit — bright colors, tropical print. Statement jewelry: oversized cocktail ring, chandelier earrings. Wedge heels. Wine glass as permanent accessory. Full makeup, slightly smudged as the night goes on.
**Vibe:** Real Housewives at a Caribbean resort. Fabulous and slightly unhinged.

### Doug "The Duke" Kaminski — The Porta Potty King
**Male | Early 50s**
Hawaiian shirt (loud, unapologetic) or golf polo. Khaki shorts or cargo shorts. White sneakers or boat shoes. Steelers gear — hat, pin, anything. Cigar (unlit). Business card holder he pulls out constantly.
**Vibe:** Calls himself "The Duke," slaps backs, insists on picking up the tab.

### Irina Volkov — The Housekeeper
**Female | Around 50**
Simple hotel uniform: black or gray polo with dark pants. Flat comfortable shoes. Hotel ID badge on lanyard. Hair pulled back — bun or low ponytail. Minimal jewelry. Master key card clipped to waist.
**Vibe:** Could walk through any room unnoticed. That's exactly the point.

### Sunny Patel — The "Consultant"
**Any Gender | Early 40s**
Forgettable business casual: khaki pants, blue or gray button-down. Conference lanyard with "Stratton Consulting Group" badge. Sensible shoes. Leather portfolio. Pen in shirt pocket.
**Vibe:** If LinkedIn became a person.

## Fortunado Marchand — The Mentalist (Host / Shane)
Velvet or sharp black blazer over a dark shirt. Optional pocket square or lapel pin for theatrical flair. Pocket watch on a chain (key prop — use it for "look into my eyes" beats). You're the stage act all night, then the man holding the room together — look like a performer who knows how to command attention without being a clown.

## General Tips
- **Conference badges for everyone:** Print "Tax Optimization Seminar — Heathens Hollow, Grand Cayman."
- **Props sell the character:** Business cards, lanyards, key cards, and drink accessories go a long way.
- **Tone:** Tropical noir. Everyone should look like they belong at an upscale Caribbean resort where something is slightly off. Sun-kissed and suspicious.
