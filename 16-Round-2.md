## SECTION B: ROUND 2 — "EVERYONE'S A SUSPECT"
### Duration: 60 Minutes

---

### OPENING: THE LOCKDOWN (0:00–0:05)
### **Everyone in the Lobby**

**HOST:** "Twenty minutes since Irina found the body. The after-party is over. The music is off. The tiki torches have been extinguished. Everyone is in the lobby. The pool still glows blue, but it doesn't look festive anymore. It looks ominous. Like a crime scene waiting to happen.

"Rosa is making calls. Security is keeping everyone in the lobby. No one's leaving. No one's going to their room. No one's making phone calls. Just waiting.

"And then Chip Davenport stands up. The retired lawyer. The guy who's been coming to this conference for thirty years. He looks at everyone.

"'We're all lawyers and criminals here. Sometimes both. Two hours before police arrive. I suggest we figure this out ourselves. Starting with: where was everybody?'"

**HOST NOTE:** Signal for alibi round. This is where the investigation begins. Each character gives their alibi. Track them on a visible board or note—have players write them down. This is the framework for everything that follows.

---

### HOST NPC: ROSA MARTINEZ (MANDATORY)

**Rosa Martinez is a mandatory host-controlled character. She is not optional. The host must play Rosa in every game — she delivers the lockdown, the master key inventory, the service entrance log, the timeline speech, and the pivotal accusation against Marco. Without her, Round 2 has no structure.**

Rosa is the night manager: late thirties, all business, the kind of competence that keeps a luxury hotel running while everyone else falls apart. Night shift manager overseeing front desk operations, staff coordination, security protocols, and hotel logistics. Knows the building layout, staff schedules, and security systems better than anyone except maintenance.

**Why NOT a Suspect:** Continuous video call with corporate office headquarters from 9 PM to 11 PM (quarterly review meeting). The call is logged and recorded by both the hotel system and corporate server. Computer screen activity shows no interruption. Witnesses on the call can confirm her presence.

**What She Knows (deliver these throughout Round 2 as scripted below):**
- Master key card logs: someone used a master key to access the service area at exactly 10:07 PM. Only four people have master keys: Rosa (locked in video call), Irina (assigned to laundry), Marco (valet), and maintenance staff (off-island). There is also an owner's override card — it bypasses the standard master key system and can access any floor or service area. Only one exists. It belongs to whoever owns this hotel.
- Val's suite has a back staircase that was propped open with a doorstop around 9:30 PM. Fire code violation. Someone deliberately kept it accessible.
- Doug arrived at midnight through the SERVICE entrance, not the front desk. In five years, no guest has ever "accidentally" used that entrance.
- The last activity on Richie's phone was incoming calls after 10 PM that triggered screen wake. He wasn't using his phone — someone was trying to reach him.

**Questions She Can Ask:**
- *To Marco, official tone:* "Marco, can you explain the master key swipe at 10:07 PM in the service corridor? Yours was checked out. Where were you, and who borrowed it?"
- *To Val, businesslike:* "Ms. Val, your back staircase was propped open with a doorstop around 9:30. Fire code violation. Who propped it open and why?"
- *To Doug, directly:* "Duke, you came through the service entrance at midnight. No guest has ever 'accidentally' used that entrance. You knew exactly where you were going. Why?"

---

### THE ALIBI ROUND (0:05–0:25)
### **Where Was Everyone Between 9 PM and 11 PM?**

**HOST:** "Let's go around the room. Where was everybody when Richie died? And before you answer, remember: we have logs. We have cameras. We have witnesses. Lies are going to get worse."

**Take each character in order. After each alibi: 1–2 minutes for questions from other players. Don't rush.**

---

#### **VAL** — *"I was in my suite."*

**Alibi:** "I was in my suite from about 9:15 until Irina screamed. Zane was with me. We were... relaxing." Hesitates slightly on the word "relaxing."

**What She Won't Say:** Crisis meeting about the crypto wallet. Zane panicking about the hardware wallet being compromised. Val trying to figure out how to liquidate assets without triggering alerts.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Hesitates when asked when she last saw Richie. "This morning? Or maybe last night?" Too vague. She's lying about something, though maybe not about the murder.

**Key Detail:** When pressed, she says Zane never left her suite. That's when players notice Zane nodding along—a little too eagerly.

---

#### **ZANE** — *"Val's suite. The whole time."*

**Alibi:** "What Val said. Her suite. The whole time. We were working on some business strategy stuff." Vague. Nervous.

**What He Won't Say:** Panicking about his hardware wallet. The wallet that supposedly contains his liquid assets. Worried that Richie knows about the wallet's location or access code.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Matches Val's alibi TOO perfectly. They've clearly discussed this. It sounds rehearsed. Two sentences. Then he stops talking.

**Key Detail:** When asked what they were "working on," both Val and Zane exchange a look. They weren't working on business. They were in crisis mode.

---

#### **MARCO** — *"At the valet stand. The whole time."*

**Alibi:** "I was at the valet stand. Working. I parked three cars between 8 and 11 PM. I've got the log sheets right here." Pulls out valet log. Defensive.

**What He Won't Say:** Took a 15-minute break around 10 PM. Someone called him. Or he met someone. He won't say who or where. Says "bathroom" but the bathroom is right by the valet stand.

**Crack in the Alibi:** "Bathroom" is a long break. When pushed on the valet log, the cars are parked but the timestamps don't add up. Three cars, three hours, but only two cars logged between 9 and 10 PM.

**Key Detail:** He's sweating. Not casual sweating. This alibi matters to him. When Richie's name is mentioned, his jaw tightens.

---

#### **DEX** — *"Behind the bar. Ask anyone."*

**Alibi:** "Behind the bar. Making drinks. I probably made two hundred drinks tonight. I took a standard twenty-minute break around 10:15 or so." Casual. Confident.

**What He Won't Say:** He brought Irina food. Coffee. Checked in on her. They have a thing going—or starting to. He went to the staff area, not just the bathroom.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Won't explain where he went during his break. Changes the subject with humor. "Gotta hydrate, you know?" But multiple witnesses can confirm he left the bar for more than five minutes.

**Key Detail:** When someone asks specifically about Irina, he looks away. That's the tell. Not guilty of murder. Guilty of something else.

---

#### **GLORIA** — *"Conference room. Discussing tax strategies."*

**Alibi:** "Conference room discussing advanced tax shelter strategies with Gerald Finch and Lorraine Chu. We vouch for each other. We were working on a presentation for tomorrow." Smooth. Prepared.

**What She Won't Say:** Gerald and Lorraine both owe her professional favors. She's reminding them with this "alibi." The meeting is real, but it's also leverage.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Gerald and Lorraine seem very eager to confirm. Maybe too eager. When pressed, they both nod before Gloria even speaks.

**Key Detail:** Gloria's hands are steady. Her voice is calm. But she checks her watch. Three times.

---

#### **PAM** — *"At the party. I think."*

**Alibi:** "I was... at the party. I think. I had a few drinks. Several drinks, actually. I'm honestly fuzzy about what happened after 9:30." Long pause. She knows this sounds bad.

**What She Won't Say:** She woke up near the laundry room. Doesn't know how she got there. Doesn't remember what she was doing. There's a vial in her pocket that she doesn't remember putting there.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Everything. Everything cracks. She can't remember anything. She looks like she's about to cry. Gloria puts an arm around her: "Leave her alone. She's grieving."

**Key Detail:** Someone notices she has a small chemical burn on her hand. She doesn't remember how she got it. That detail lands like a bomb.

---

#### **DOUG** — *"I wasn't even here!"*

**Alibi:** "I wasn't even HERE until midnight! I was in Pittsburgh dealing with a porta potty situation. I've got texts, call logs, the whole nine yards." Pulls out his phone. Defensive immediately.

**What He Won't Say:** He arrived through the service entrance. Not the front. He was on the island earlier. He lied about when he arrived.

**Crack in the Alibi:** His phone shows calls and texts, but not location. Location data can be spoofed. The calls could be from anywhere. When Rosa mentions the service entrance, he goes pale.

**Key Detail:** Four hours unaccounted for. From 8 PM to midnight. He says he was in Pittsburgh, but what if he wasn't? What if he arrived early?

---

#### **IRINA** — *"I was working. Laundry. All night."*

**Alibi:** "[Halting English] I am working. Laundry. All night. This is my job. I find him. I am not... I did not..." Voice shaking. Nearly incoherent.

**What She Won't Say:** She delivered a garment bag to Richie's floor at 7 PM. She was in the laundry room working. She found him at 10:45 PM. She's terrified.

**Crack in the Alibi:** Her alibi and her opportunity are identical. She had access. She was there. She found the body. But did she find him, or did she leave him?

**Key Detail:** Rosa mentions the master key was used at 10:07 PM. Irina has a master key. She doesn't answer.

---

#### **SUNNY** — *"At the party. The whole time."*

**Alibi:** "I was at the party the whole time. Networking. I spoke with Val, then Marco, then Dex, then Pam. Very visible. Multiple witnesses." Lists several names. Smooth.

**What She Won't Say:** She's been observing everyone as part of her job. She knows more than she's saying. She was taking notes—mental notes.

**Crack in the Alibi:** So thorough it sounds rehearsed. She accounts for every minute. Who actually does that? Who remembers exactly where they were every second?

**Key Detail:** When asked about specifics—"What did you talk to Val about?"—she hesitates for the first time. That hesitation is everything.

---

### SECRETS SURFACE (0:25–0:45)
### **The Cracks Widen**

Space these revelations 3–5 minutes apart. Let tension build between disclosures. These are hammer blows, not gentle questions.

---

#### **REVELATION 1: Val & Zane's "Business" (3 minutes in)**

Someone notices Val and Zane are very close. Sitting together. Communicating silently. Someone asks: "Are you two... together?"

Val laughs. "Business partners. Crypto consulting. We're trying to diversify Richie's portfolio." Zane nods. But the answer is too quick.

Pushed further: "What does 'diversify Richie's portfolio' mean?"

**Moment of tension.** Val: "Asset management. Enterprise holdings. Standard wealth strategy."

But then **Fletch** chimes in (if present, or HOST mentions): "Val, I got great shots of you and Richie on the beach yesterday. You two seemed... intense. Want me to delete those?"

Val's face goes hard. "Where are those photos?"

This is the moment players realize: Val and Zane are in a business crisis. Money is missing. Or being moved. Or Richie was threatening to expose something.

**EVIDENCE DROP:** Fletch has photos of Val and Richie arguing. Val crying. Richie pointing.

---

#### **REVELATION 2: The Car Scratch (7 minutes in)**

Someone mentions the valet incident. Marco's face changes. He gets defensive.

"Richie called me a 'local punk' over a scratch on his stupid car. A SCRATCH. I don't know why he was so upset about a scratch."

The anger is disproportionate. Someone presses: "You were angry at him?"

Marco: "Wouldn't you be? He called me names. Over a parking lot accident."

Doug: "But you were really angry, right? More than just about the car."

Marco doesn't answer.

**EVIDENCE DROP:** Rosa has an incident report. Richie called Marco "a local punk" in front of guests. Marco "just stared" at him. The look, according to the report, was "unsettling."

Someone asks Rosa: "What did Marco look like when Richie called him that?"

Rosa: "Like he was memorizing Richie's face. Like he was deciding something."

---

#### **REVELATION 3: Pam's Drunken State (11 minutes in)**

Destiny (if present, or HOST mentions): "I got video of you getting progressively drunker. You went from 'fun aunt' to 'scary aunt' in two hours."

Pam looks mortified. "Don't show that to anyone."

Destiny: "You said something about making Richie pay. What did you mean by that?"

Pam's face goes white.

**EVIDENCE DROP:** Text message from Richie to Pam (found on Richie's phone): "We need to talk about last night. I can't keep doing this. We're done. I mean it this time."

Gloria reads it aloud. "We're done. I can't keep doing this. What was 'this,' Pam?"

Pam cries. Won't answer. She and Richie were having an affair. Or something. Something she wanted to end, or he wanted to end, or both.

---

#### **REVELATION 4: The Margarita Incident (15 minutes in)**

Zane mentions bitterly: "Remember when Richie ordered a margarita at the bar and Dex 'accidentally' spilled it on him?"

Val laughs nervously. "That happened last night, at dinner."

Zane: "Yeah. Richie went on a rant about it. Called Dex incompetent. But Dex looked at me like... like he meant to do it."

Someone asks Dex directly: "Did you spill that margarita on purpose?"

Dex smiles. Doesn't answer. That's the answer.

Chip (if present, or HOST): "That seemed deliberate. Richie doesn't stumble. He would have had to be standing there to get that much spillage. Dex was standing right next to him."

**EVIDENCE DROP:** A waitress confirms: Dex said something to Richie before the spill. She didn't hear what, but Richie's face changed, and then the margarita went everywhere.

Zane adds, more quietly: "And then at the lobby bar, Richie knocked my margarita straight onto my jacket. Soaked my hardware wallet. Twice in two days. I started thinking it wasn't clumsiness."

---

#### **REVELATION 5: Dex's Evasiveness (19 minutes in)**

Someone pushes Dex on his 20-minute break. "Where did you actually go?"

Dex deflects with humor: "Gotta hydrate, man. The bar is hot."

Doug: "Dex, buddy. I've known you long enough to know when you're full of it. Where did you go?"

**Moment.** This is when players realize: Dex and Doug have history. They know each other. This is the first time this comes out.

Dex looks at Doug. Something passes between them. "I wasn't—I didn't do anything during my break."

Doug: "But you weren't behind the bar. Multiple people have said you disappeared."

Dex: "I took a break. That's what people do."

But now the question is: Dex knows Doug. Doug knows Dex. Why are they here together?

**EVIDENCE DROP:** Someone recognizes them now. "Wait, do you two know each other?"

Uncomfortable silence.

---

#### **REVELATION 6: The Timeline (23 minutes in)**

Rosa stands up. "Let's talk about the timeline. Richie was alive at 8 PM. He ordered vodka at 7 PM, sent to his room. He was scheduled to give a toast at 9 PM. He didn't show. Irina found him at 10:45 PM. That's a two-hour window. The last activity on Richie's phone was incoming calls after 10 PM that triggered screen wake. He wasn't using his phone — someone was trying to reach him. Where was everyone between 10 and 10:30?"

**Room goes quiet.**

Rosa: "A master key was used to access the service area at 10:07 PM. Only four people have master keys. Me—I was on a video call with corporate. Irina—she was in the laundry. Marco—his key was checked out. And maintenance—he's off-island. There is also an owner's override card — it bypasses the standard master key system and can access any floor or service area. Only one exists. It belongs to whoever owns this hotel."

She looks at Marco. "So, Marco, where was your master key at 10:07 PM?"

Marco: "I... I was moving a car into the service garage. Routine valet task. It's logged in my records."

Rosa: "And you used your master key to access the service area for that?"

Marco: "Yes. To move the car. That's it."

Rosa: "Then why do you look like you're about to be sick?"

Marco doesn't answer.

---

### FIRST ACCUSATIONS (0:45–0:55)
### **Theories Collide**

Let accusations happen naturally from the players. Guide if needed, but let them lead.

---

#### **ACCUSATION 1: Marco**

Someone says it: "The car scratch. The master key. The 15 unaccounted minutes. Marco had motive and opportunity."

Marco: "I didn't kill anyone over a CAR SCRATCH."

Zane: "But you were angry. You 'just stared' at him."

Marco: "Lots of people were angry at Richie. That doesn't make me a murderer."

**HOST NOTE:** Marco is telling the truth here. He wasn't murdered over the car scratch. The real motive is much bigger. But he knows something. He's hiding something. That something isn't murder—it's complicity.

---

#### **ACCUSATION 2: Pam**

Someone says it: "She can't remember anything. She was with Richie the night before, fighting. There's a text from him saying they're done. And she's got a chemical burn on her hand. She had motive and opportunity."

Pam cries. Gloria puts her arm around her: "Leave her alone. She's grieving."

But even Gloria looks worried.

**HOST NOTE:** Pam is innocent of murder. But she was in the laundry room. She doesn't remember how she got there. There's something else happening with Pam—something involving blackmail, or coercion, or setup.

---

#### **ACCUSATION 3: The Alibi Twins**

Someone: "Val and Zane were 'together' for three hours. Doing what? Sleeping? Having sex? Or planning to move a body?"

Val: "That's our business."

Zane nods too quickly. "Yeah. Our business."

Someone: "Your business involves Richie's money, doesn't it? His hardware wallet? The crypto assets?"

**Moment of tension.** Val and Zane exchange a look. They're considering lying. They're considering staying silent. Then:

Val: "We weren't killing Richie. We need him alive to move the assets."

**This lands differently.** They're not denying motive. They're saying their motive requires Richie alive. That's credible.

Someone: "Then what were you doing in your suite for three hours?"

Val: "I told you. Trying to figure out how to solve a problem. Without involving Richie."

Zane: "But we didn't solve it. We just... waited. Waited to see if Richie would show up."

**HOST NOTE:** Val and Zane are telling the truth — unless you are running Path 1, in which case Val left for approximately 20 minutes around 10 PM and Zane will only admit this under pressure in Round 3. In all other paths, their alibi holds. Either way, their alibi makes them look guilty of something else. That's the story. That's the tension.

---

### CLOSING BEAT (0:55–1:00)
### **The Realization**

**HOST:** "Police sirens in the distance. Getting closer. The sound of sirens makes everyone quiet.

"In the silence, someone says: 'We're missing something. This isn't just about who hated Richie. It's about who had the most to lose.'

"And that's when it hits everyone: they ALL had something to lose. Val and Zane's money. Pam's reputation. Marco's job. Dex's secret relationship. Irina's job. Sunny's cover. Doug's... whatever Doug's dealing with. Gloria's perfect image. Zane's cryptocurrency.

"Everyone had a reason to want Richie dead. And now one of them—maybe one of them in this room—actually did it."

**HOST:** "End of Round 2. Take a ten-minute break. Grab water. Breathe.

"When we come back, the investigation gets real. The police arrive. The questions get harder. And the game—the real game—begins.

"Someone in this room is a killer. And they're good enough to make it look like chaos."
