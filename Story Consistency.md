- guidance
	- 1. Come up with means, motive, opportunity and a distinct feature of the killer(left handed, victim knew them, unusual tattoo etc). I'll refer to those facts as "clues" from now.
    
2. Create a list of potential, named suspects. One of the clues should apply to all of them and **only them**. ("They were all at that party", "They would have access to that kind of poison", "They all wanted them dead for the same/different reasons", "They're all members of the same gang and therefore they have that tattoo" .etc). Will call it **"limit clue",** as it's purpose it to limit the scope of investigation to manageable size.
    
3. All of those clues apply to the actual killer, while the rest of the suspects match the limiting clue and another one. Even if the players only manage to find 2 out of 4, they'll still be able to figure it out just by looking deeper into 2 suspects. However, the investigation won't end the second they find something just because it's obvious from just one clue.
