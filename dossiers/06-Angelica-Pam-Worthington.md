## 1. Your Costume

**Female | Mid 40s**
Bold resort dress or jumpsuit — bright colors, tropical print. Statement jewelry: oversized cocktail ring, chandelier earrings. Wedge heels. Wine glass as permanent accessory. Full makeup, slightly smudged as the night goes on.
**Vibe:** Real Housewives at a Caribbean resort. Fabulous and slightly unhinged.

---

## 2. Your Props

Wine glass (permanent), chemical vial (small bottle with yellowish powder — flour or cornstarch)

## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Female | Mid 40s | SUSPECT

You came to the Caymans to reinvent yourself. Resort wear that's trying a little too hard. Jewelry that announces recent alimony. You joke about finding your next spouse, but underneath the bravado you're running out of money and running out of time. You drink too much and you know it. You're fabulous and slightly unhinged — Real Housewives energy at a Caribbean resort.

### Your 3 Secrets

1. **Your first husband died under suspicious circumstances.** A fall at the lake house, four years ago. You were blackout drunk. The investigation was closed by family lawyers, but the bruising didn't match an accident. You're terrified you killed him and don't remember.
2. **You blacked out again tonight.** Around 9:45 PM, everything goes fuzzy. You woke up near the laundry room at 11:30 with a small vial of chemical residue in your hand. You don't know how you got there or what the vial is.
3. **Paloma made a cruel comment about you the night before** — something about your age, your desperation, how pathetic you are. You were furious. You told several people you'd "make her pay." You had a complicated relationship with Paloma — attraction, resentment, obsession, all tangled together.

### Your Alibi

You were at the party. You think. You had a lot of drinks. You honestly can't remember much after 9:30 PM. You have a chemical burn on your hand you can't explain. You can barely account for two hours of your night. This looks terrible and you know it.

### Your Connections

- **Paloma** — Complicated. Attraction and resentment. She hurt you and you couldn't let it go.
- **Gloria** — Your genuine best friend. She covers for your blackouts and puts her arm around you when you cry.
- **Irina** — You may have seen her near the laundry during your blackout. Or maybe you imagined it.
