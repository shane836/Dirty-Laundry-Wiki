## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Female | Late 30s | NPC (Host plays or assigns as small speaking role)

Night shift manager at Heathens Hollow. All business, crisp competence. You oversee front desk operations, staff coordination, security protocols, and hotel logistics. You know the building layout, staff schedules, and security systems better than anyone. You've managed worse than this — drunk weddings, bachelorette meltdowns, two norovirus outbreaks — but never a body. You speak in clean sentences, you do not editorialize, and you do not lie to authority. You also do not enjoy being lied to.

### Why You're Not a Suspect

Continuous video call with corporate headquarters from 9 to 11 PM — quarterly review meeting. The call is logged and recorded. Your screen activity shows no interruption. Sasha at the front desk and the call timestamps put you in the manager's office for the entire kill window.

### What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- **The master-key audit doesn't reconcile.** Four master keys are supposed to be checked in nightly — yours, Irina's, Marco's, and maintenance's. Pepe was supposed to drop his at shift end and didn't (he forgot it in his toolbox). That means there is **one more master key floating around than the log shows**, and you can't account for which key opened the service area at 10:07 PM. "I run a tight key audit. Tonight, my count is off by one. That has never happened on my watch."
- **Irina's 7 PM garment-bag run was not a normal route.** Garment service goes through the bell desk, not laundry. Irina took the bag herself, used the service stairs instead of the guest elevator, and was off-camera for almost eleven minutes between the laundry and Paloma's floor. "Laundry doesn't deliver garments. I would have authorized that, and I didn't."
- **The service stairwell side exit was propped open from 6 PM.** Pepe filed it. You did not get to it before your call started. "Fire-code violation. Whoever wedged that door wanted a way in or out that wouldn't show on the keycard logs. That's not housekeeping. That's pre-meditation."
- **Doug entered through the service entrance at 9:45 PM, not midnight.** Sasha logged a man matching his description coming in the back way. He didn't sign in until 12:00 AM at the front desk. "Mr. Kaminski's 'official' arrival is two hours and fifteen minutes after his actual one. No guest uses that entrance by accident."
- **The owner's override card swiped Paloma's floor at 10:05 PM.** Only one override exists, and it lives with Val. But Val also signed a paper maintenance work order at 10:05 PM for a leaking unit on Floor 3 — Pepe has the carbon copy. The card and the signature don't put her in the same place. "Either Val was on two floors at once, or somebody else had her override card."
- **Val's back staircase was wedged open at 9:30 PM** — same MO as the side exit. Same person, probably. "Two propped doors in one night, both on routes that bypass the front desk. Somebody mapped this hotel before they came in."
- **You filed an incident report on Marco a week ago.** Ms. Rathbone called him a "local punk" in front of guests over a scratch on her rental car. The comments box has a heavy black scratch where Marco signed in anger — you keep the carbon. "I document every guest-staff incident. That one was uglier than most. He didn't forget it. Neither did I."
- **Pepe filed a missing-supplies report two days ago** — cleaning compounds out of the maintenance closet, the kind a chemist could repurpose. It went into your queue and you hadn't followed up on it before tonight. "I should have escalated that. I didn't. That's on me, and it's going to come up when I write this up for corporate."
