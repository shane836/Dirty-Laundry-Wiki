## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Any Gender | Early 40s | SUSPECT

The least memorable person at the conference. Management consultant from "Stratton Consulting Group." You talk in corporate buzzwords — synergy, optimization, stakeholder alignment. Forgettable suit, aggressive blandness. You're a LinkedIn profile that became a person. Nobody remembers your face. That's exactly how you trained it.

### Your 3 Secrets

1. **You're not a consultant.** You're an undercover IRS/Interpol agent on a joint task force. You've been embedded at this conference for six months, posing as a management consulting firm to build cases against everyone in the room.
2. **Paloma was your star informant.** She was set to testify in a matter of weeks — enough evidence to bring down Gloria's cartel, Val's trafficking operation, and half the white-collar criminals at this conference. Her death didn't just end a life. It destroyed years of investigation and your career.
3. **You misspent federal expense money on personal luxuries** instead of funding proper witness security for Paloma. If your supervisors find out, your career is over regardless. You carry guilt about whether better security could have saved her.

### Your Alibi

You were at the party the whole time. Networking. You can list every person you spoke to and when — Val, then Marco, then Dex, then Pam. Very visible. Multiple witnesses. But your alibi is so thorough it sounds rehearsed. Who actually tracks every minute of their evening? Someone trained to construct an alibi, that's who. Professional invisibility makes a perfect cover — through misdirection.

### Your Connections

- **Paloma** — Your informant. Quasi-friendship. You feel guilty about her death.
- **Val** — She hired your fake firm for a hotel audit. She's an investigation target and doesn't know it.
- **Gloria** — Your primary RICO target. Her cartel structure is fully mapped in your files.
- **Zane** — He suspects you're law enforcement. He's been watching you.
