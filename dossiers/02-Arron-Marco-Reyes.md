## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Male | Early 20s | SUSPECT

You're the hotel's head valet. Young, handsome, charming with every guest. You remember drink orders and dog names. You work hard and keep your head down — or that's the version everyone sees. In reality, you've been running small-time drug deals through the valet stand and skimming cash for years, building toward the day you disappear.

### Your 3 Secrets

1. **Gloria Reyes is your mother.** Nobody at this party knows that. You and Gloria have been careful to keep distance in public. She placed you at this hotel to keep an eye on things — but you have your own plans.
2. **You have a go-bag hidden in your room** — cash, a fake passport, a burner phone. You're planning to disappear. You've been saving for years and you're almost ready to leave this life behind.
3. **Paloma's bad intelligence got your brother Diego killed two years ago.** A cartel ambush. Diego was 22. Paloma called it "cost of doing business." You've been carrying that rage every day since.

### Your Alibi

You were at the valet stand all night. Multiple guests saw you. You have log sheets showing cars parked. But you took a 15-minute break around 10 PM that you can't fully explain, and your master key was used to access the service area at 10:07 PM. You say you were moving a car into the service garage. That's technically true. But you saw something in the service corridor you haven't told anyone about.

### Your Connections

- **Gloria** — Your mother. You love her but you're planning to leave without telling her.
- **Paloma** — She got your brother killed. You hate her.
- **Irina** — You two have a thing. She's the only person who knows the real you.
- **Dex** — He recruited you into this life when you were 19. He carries guilt about it.
- **Val** — Your boss. You have a small crush.
