## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Male | Early 50s | MINOR CHARACTER (Not a suspect)

Tax attorney from Houston. You've been doing this for decades — helping people shelter money, structure trusts, and navigate the gray areas of international finance. You're good at your job and bad at keeping your cool under pressure. Tonight you're sweating through your shirt, and it's not the Caribbean heat.

### Why You're Not a Suspect

You were in the conference room from 8 PM onward, confirmed by Lorraine, hotel staff, and security footage of the corridor. You didn't leave.

### What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- You were in the conference room with Gloria and Lorraine all evening, discussing tax strategies. Gloria ran the meeting — she always does.
- **Gloria stepped out around 10:25 PM.** She said restroom. You didn't think anything of it. But it was closer to ten minutes, not five. Under enough pressure, you'll admit this. You'll crack before Lorraine does — you're a worse liar.
- You owe Gloria favors. She's helped your firm with referrals and connections. Contradicting her feels dangerous in ways you don't fully understand but instinctively respect.
- Gloria's composure tonight has been unsettling. Everyone else is rattled. Gloria is steady. That bothers you.

### Questions You Can Ask

- *To the room, sweating:* "Look, I was in the conference room the whole time. We all were. Gloria was there. She was... mostly there."
- *To Lorraine, under breath:* "I can't keep covering. I'm not going to prison for this."
