## Who You Are

Big man, big laugh, big presence. You run Kaminski's Portable Sanitation — KPS — out of Pittsburgh. You call yourself "The Duke" and nobody's going to stop you. You make porta potty jokes constantly and never get tired of them. Hawaiian shirt, Steelers gear, cigar in hand. You're the guy at every event who slaps backs and insists on picking up the tab. Underneath the act, you're sharper than anyone gives you credit for.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **Your porta potty business is a money-laundering front.** You wash serious cash through KPS for Gloria's cartel operation every month. The portable sanitation empire runs on dirty money.
2. **You weren't in Pittsburgh tonight.** You fabricated the porta potty emergency — pre-arranged calls and texts through your office. You actually arrived at the hotel through the service entrance hours before your "official" midnight arrival. Four hours unaccounted for.
3. **You have a protective crush on Val.** She has no idea. You'd do almost anything to keep her out of trouble. You don't know about her trafficking operation — you just think she's a beautiful woman running a hotel.

## Your Alibi

You claim you were in Pittsburgh dealing with a porta potty emergency until midnight. You have texts, call logs, the whole nine yards. But your phone shows calls and texts — not location. The calls could be from anywhere. And Rosa can confirm you came through the service entrance, not the front desk. No guest has ever "accidentally" used that entrance.

## Your Connections

- **Gloria** — She's your business partner in the laundering operation. You have a mild flirtation.
- **Val** — Protective crush. You don't know about the trafficking.
- **Dex** — Old friends from a previous life. Neither of you has mentioned this to anyone.
- **Pam** — Mild flirtation. Your backup plan.
