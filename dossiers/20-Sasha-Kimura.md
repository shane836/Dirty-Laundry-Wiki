## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Female | Mid 20s | MINOR CHARACTER (Not a suspect)

Paloma Rathbone's personal assistant and right hand. Young, overwhelmed, way in over your head. You've been managing conference logistics, handling Paloma's schedule, fielding her calls, and pretending everything was normal when it very clearly wasn't. You know more about Paloma's state of mind in her final days than anyone.

### Why You're Not a Suspect

You were at the front desk handling conference wrap-up logistics from 7 PM onward. Rosa and front desk staff confirm your location continuously. You were buried in paperwork and phone calls.

### What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- **Paloma was acting erratic all week.** Paranoid. Drinking more than usual — and her usual was a lot. Making encrypted phone calls she didn't want you to hear. "She kept looking over her shoulder. She wasn't sleeping."
- **Paloma gave you a sealed envelope two days ago.** She said: "If anything happens to me, give this to the authorities." You still have it. Inside: a USB drive. You haven't opened it. "She looked scared when she gave it to me. Not sad — scared."
- You noticed **Paloma and Val had a tense interaction** the day before the murder. Not the beach argument — a separate, quieter one in the lobby. "Val grabbed her arm. Paloma pulled away. It looked personal."
- Paloma mentioned someone named **Diego** once, in passing, a few days ago. "She said the name like it hurt. I didn't ask."

### Questions You Can Ask

- *To the Mentalist:* "Paloma gave me this envelope. She said to give it to the authorities if anything happened. Should I open it?"
- *To Val:* "What happened between you and Paloma in the lobby yesterday? She wouldn't talk about it after."
- *To Sunny:* "Paloma got a lot of calls this week from a blocked number. Was that you?"
