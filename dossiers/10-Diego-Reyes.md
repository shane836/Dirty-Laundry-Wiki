# Diego Reyes (Ghost) Game Packet

## 1. Your Costume

**Male | Early 20s**
Wear whatever you'd wear to a party. You're a ghost — the contrast between casual clothes and your eerie presence is the whole effect. Optional: all white, or notably out of place.

## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Male | Age 22 (deceased) | MINOR CHARACTER (Ghost)

### Your Situation

You are dead. Not tonight — two years ago. You were killed in a cartel ambush at age 22. Bad intelligence from Paloma Rathbone got you set up. She called it "cost of doing business."

You are Gloria's firstborn son and Marco's older brother. Tonight you're a ghost at a party full of people connected to your death. You don't interact with the living in a normal way — you drift through the room, you observe, you say things that land heavy. You're the emotional weight of this story.

### How to Play a Ghost

- You don't introduce yourself normally. If someone asks who you are, you say your name and nothing else. Let it be unsettling.
- You can comment on what's happening, but from a removed perspective. "She always did that." "He used to bring me to parties like this."
- You don't accuse anyone. You don't solve the mystery. You're not here for justice — you're here because the story can't be told without you.
- When your death comes up in Round 3, that's your moment. You can tell the room what happened — the ambush, the bad intel, the phone call that never came.
- You can be near Gloria without acknowledging her as your mother until the reveal. Then the look between you says everything.

### What You Know

- Gloria is your mother. Marco is your brother.
- Paloma gave intelligence to a rival cartel that led to the ambush that killed you.
- Gloria has been carrying your death for two years. It changed her.
- Marco has been carrying your death too — it's why he's angry, why he has a go-bag, why he flinches at Paloma's name.

### Your Round Beats

- **Round 1:** You're just present. Drifting. People might not even notice you. That's fine.
- **Round 2:** If someone asks about you, they learn you're "someone who used to be here." Cryptic.
- **Round 3:** Your story comes out. This is your moment. Zoya or another character reveals Gloria's family history. You can tell it from your side.
- **Round 4:** You watch your mother confess. You're the reason she did it.
