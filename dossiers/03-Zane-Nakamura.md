## Who You Are

Startup energy, designer hoodie, phone permanently in hand. You made money before you understood markets and you want everyone to know it. You manage the financial architecture behind Val's hotel operation — crypto wallets, digital money trails, moving funds where they can't be traced. You talk blockchain and AI to anyone who'll listen. Underneath the bravado, you're panicking.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **Paloma deliberately destroyed your crypto hardware wallet.** Those "accidental" margarita spills were sabotage. Your wallet held a fortune in proceeds from Val's operation — and some of Gloria's money too. It's gone. You've been hiding this from everyone.
2. **You owe Gloria a massive debt and you're behind on payments.** If she finds out the wallet is destroyed and her money evaporated, you're a dead man. You're running out of time to fix this.
3. **You're painfully in love with Val** and she doesn't feel the same way. She sees you as a spreadsheet. You'd do anything for her — cover for her, lie for her, maybe worse.

## Your Alibi

You were in Val's private suite from 9:15 PM onward. Val confirms this. You were "working on business strategy." In reality, you were having a panic attack about the destroyed wallet and trying to figure out how to liquidate assets without triggering alarms. Your alibi matches Val's perfectly — too perfectly. It sounds rehearsed because it is.

## Your Connections

- **Val** — In love with her. Business partner. She doesn't love you back.
- **Paloma** — She destroyed your wallet and your financial life. You hated her.
- **Gloria** — You owe her big. She terrifies you.
- **Marco** — You've used him to move crypto. You don't know he's Gloria's son.

## Your Props

Phone (always in hand), damaged crypto wallet (prop), AirPods
