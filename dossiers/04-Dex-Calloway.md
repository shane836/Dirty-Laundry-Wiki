## Who You Are

You tend bar at Heathens Hollow. You make perfect cocktails, quote philosophers between pours, and read people better than anyone in the room. You're way too smart and too well-read to be "just a bartender." That's because you aren't. You had a whole other life before this one, and you left it behind — mostly.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **Your real name is Dexter Villareal.** You come from old money — the Calloway family of Greenwich, Connecticut. You disowned them (or they disowned you, depending on who's telling the story). Nobody here knows your real background.
2. **You spent years as a cartel fixer for Gloria's organization.** You ran assets, solved problems, and did things you don't talk about. You got out — but Gloria still has leverage over you. She could drag you back anytime.
3. **You're secretly in love with Irina.** You bring her coffee every morning. You've been learning Russian. She's the reason you haven't left the island. You don't know she's involved with Marco.

## Your Alibi

You were behind the bar all night. Dozens of guests saw you. You took a 20-minute break around 10:15 — won't explain where you went. (The truth: you were bringing Irina food in the staff area. Admitting this reveals your relationship with her and her connection to the cartel.) You deflect with humor when pressed. "Gotta hydrate, man."

## Your Connections

- **Irina** — In love with her. Brings her coffee daily. Doesn't know about her and Marco.
- **Gloria** — Former boss. She has leverage. You're not free.
- **Marco** — You recruited him into this life when he was 19. You carry guilt about that.
- **Doug** — Old friends from a previous life. You know each other but neither of you has mentioned it.
- **Pam** — Casual acquaintance. She owes you from a poker game.

## Your Props

Bar apron, bar towel, cocktail shaker or bar tools
