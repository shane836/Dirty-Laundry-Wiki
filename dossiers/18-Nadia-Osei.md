## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Female | Late 20s | MINOR CHARACTER (Not a suspect)

You run the music and entertainment for the after-party. Headphones around your neck, playlist queued, vibes on point. You've been at the DJ booth near the pool all night — you've got a full view of the courtyard, the entrances, and who's coming and going. You're observant because good DJs read the room.

### Why You're Not a Suspect

At the DJ booth all night, visible to dozens of guests. You didn't leave the outdoor area. Multiple witnesses and your own continuous playlist timestamps confirm your location.

### What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- You saw **Doug arrive through the service entrance at 9:45 PM** — not midnight like he claims. "He came in the back way, looked around like he didn't want to be seen, then disappeared into the main building. I didn't think much of it until... well, now."
- You saw **someone on the back staircase from Val's suite floor** around 10 PM. Couldn't tell who — it was dark and you only caught a silhouette. "Someone was definitely using that staircase."
- You heard **Gloria speaking rapid Spanish on the phone** around 8 PM. "I don't speak much Spanish, but her tone was serious. Not party talk. She looked stressed."
- The music was loud enough that people near the pool couldn't have heard a scream from the service area. "If someone screamed in the laundry room before 10:30, nobody out here would've heard it."

### Questions You Can Ask

- *To Doug:* "Hey Duke, I saw you come in the back way around 9:45. I thought you were in Pittsburgh?"
- *To Val:* "Someone was on your back staircase around 10. Any idea who?"
