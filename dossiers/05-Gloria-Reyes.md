## Who You Are

You look like you chair the PTA. Linen dress, perfect blowout, understated jewelry. You ask about people's families, remember their kids' names, and speak so softly people lean in to hear you. You bring deviled eggs to potlucks and serve on charity committees. Nobody suspects the soccer mom from suburban Tampa.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **You run a cartel operation spanning Florida and the Caribbean.** The soccer mom thing is a cover. You're a serious mid-level operator — drugs, money laundering, leverage. Several people at this party work for you whether they know it or not.
2. **You ordered Irina to poison Paloma's shirt.** You gave Irina a slow-acting toxin and told her to lace Paloma's dress shirt through the hotel laundry. The shirt was the plan — remote, deniable, cold. But when you saw Paloma still alive at 10:25 PM, you went yourself with the insulin pen.
3. **Your son Diego was killed two years ago because of Paloma.** She fed bad intelligence to a rival cartel. Diego was 22. She called it "cost of doing business." You've been planning this since the day you buried him.

## Your Alibi

You were in the conference room all evening with Gerald Finch and Lorraine Chu, discussing tax strategies. They'll both confirm — they owe you favors. In reality, you stepped out for about ten minutes around 10:25 PM through the side exit. Three minutes to the service area. You found Paloma. You finished what the shirt started. You were back before anyone questioned your absence.

## Your Connections

- **Marco** — Your son. Nobody knows. You placed him at this hotel. You'd do anything for him.
- **Diego** — Your firstborn. Dead. The reason you're doing this.
- **Irina** — Your operative. She follows your orders because you pay for her mother's medical care and her daughter's school.
- **Dex** — Former fixer. You have leverage over him.
- **Pam** — Your genuine best friend. She doesn't know about the cartel.
- **Doug** — He launders money for you through his porta potty business.

## 7. Round 4 — "The Reveal"
*In-game time: Midnight*

### IF GLORIA IS CORRECTLY IDENTIFIED:

### GLORIA REYES — The Confession
- Don't run. Don't deflect. Stand up. Look at Marco.
- Deliver the confession (from memory, paraphrased, or read from a provided card):

"Yes. I killed her.

The shirt was always the plan. Remote. Clean. I gave Irina the compound. She delivered it through the laundry. Paloma would get sick slowly — by morning it would look like a medical event. That was the professional plan.

But at 10:25, I saw her through the conference room door. Stumbling. Grabbing the wall. Still alive. The shirt should have been enough.

But I couldn't sit in that room and wait.

I found her near the laundry. She recognized me. 'Gloria? What are you—'

I injected the paralytic. Ninety seconds.

The shirt was the operation. The injection was me. A mother.

My son Diego was twenty-two. Paloma gave one piece of bad information to the wrong people and called it cost of doing business. She never lost a night of sleep. I've lost every night for two years.

She was going to testify. She was going to bring down everyone in this room — including Marco, who has done nothing except be born into the wrong family.

A mother protects her children. Even the dead ones."

*Looks at Marco.* "I'm sorry you had to see this."
