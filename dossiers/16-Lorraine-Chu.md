## 3. Your Character

**Who You Are:** Female | Late 40s | MINOR CHARACTER (Not a suspect)

Senior tax-strategy partner and CPA out of San Francisco. You build the structures the rich live inside — trusts, holding companies, offshore vehicles, the legal scaffolding that keeps wealth quiet. You're better at this than Gerald, calmer than Gerald, and a much, much better liar than Gerald. You speak slowly. You answer questions with questions. You let other people fill silences. You are not flustered tonight, but you are *measuring* every word — you know exactly how much trouble Gloria is in, and you know exactly how much you can say before you walk into it with her.

### Why You're Not a Suspect

You were in the conference room with Gloria and Gerald from 8 PM onward, working through tax-strategy slides. Confirmed by Gerald, hotel staff, and the corridor camera. You did not leave the room.

### What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- You were in the conference room with Gloria and Gerald all night. The meeting was Gloria's — she ran the agenda, she set the pace. Even tonight, after a body was found, Gloria has been the steadiest person in the room. **That is not normal.** "Everyone here is rattled. Gloria is composed. I've known her fifteen years. I have never seen her this composed."
- **Gloria stepped out around 10:25 PM.** Said she was going to the restroom. You looked at your watch when she left and again when she came back. It was closer to **ten or eleven minutes**, not five. The nearest restroom is forty seconds away. Gerald didn't notice. You noticed. You'll admit it under direct questioning, but you will not volunteer it. "I keep time professionally. She was gone longer than a restroom break. I don't know where she went. I do know how long."
- **Gloria has been pressuring you for years.** Favors. Referrals. A few "introductions" you should not have made. She has documentation of those introductions. You know this because she's reminded you about it twice this week. "I am not going to prison for Gloria Reyes. I will testify to exactly what I saw. I will not embellish, and I will not protect her."
- **Gloria's tax-shelter clients overlap suspiciously with people at this party.** You've seen the same shell entities show up on Doug Kaminski's portfolio, on a structure tied to Val Montecito's hotel group, and on two trusts you suspect are Marco-adjacent. Paloma Rathbone was the only person who could draw a line through all of those names on one piece of paper. **And on Monday she was supposed to.**
- **Gloria took a phone call around 8 PM** in the courtyard, before the conference-room session started. Rapid Spanish, low voice, very tense. She came back in and her hand was shaking. It steadied within thirty seconds. "I do not speak Spanish. I speak fluent body language. Whatever that call was, it was operational, not personal."
- You watched **Gerald check his watch four times in ten minutes** right before Gloria stepped out at 10:25. He wasn't checking his own watch. He was watching *Gloria* check hers. He's been protecting her all night. He's about to crack and he doesn't know it. "Gerald is going to talk. The only question is whether he does it tonight or after the police arrive."
