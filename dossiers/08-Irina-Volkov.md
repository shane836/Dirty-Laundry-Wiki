## 3. Your Character

**Female | Around 50 | SUSPECT**

---

## Who You Are

Head of housekeeping at Heathens Hollow. Quiet, efficient, invisible by design. You speak with a Russian accent — halting English that makes people underestimate you. You manage the cleaning staff, the laundry systems, the service corridors. Guests forget you exist ten minutes after meeting you. That's exactly how you want it.

## Your 3 Secrets

1. **You are Gloria's operative.** Not just a housekeeper — a handler. Gloria placed you at this hotel to manage her interests. You report to her. You follow her orders. You have no choice.
2. **You prepared and delivered the poisoned shirt.** Gloria gave you the chemical compound. You laced Paloma's dress shirt and routed it through normal hotel laundry to her room at 7 PM. You did what you were told.
3. **Paloma was the only person here who was kind to you.** She spoke Russian with you. She remembered your name. She brought you poetry. She treated you like a person, not furniture. You helped kill the only person who saw you.

## Your Alibi

You were working laundry all night. Staff saw you. This is your job — you're expected to be there. But being in the laundry area is also exactly where you'd be if you were delivering a poisoned shirt. Your alibi and your opportunity are the same thing. You found the body at 10:45 PM and screamed. Or did you already know it was there?

## Your Connections

- **Gloria** — Your boss. She pays for your mother's cancer treatment in Moscow and your daughter's school in St. Petersburg. Without her money, they have nothing. That's her leverage.
- **Marco** — You have a thing with him. Secret, real, and probably doomed. He's the only person who knows the real you.
- **Dex** — He brings you coffee every morning. He's learning Russian. He's in love with you. You know this. You haven't told him about Marco.
- **Paloma** — She was kind to you. You killed her anyway.

## Your Props

Hotel ID badge on lanyard, master key card (labeled), cleaning cloth in pocket
