# Round 4 Cards — "The Reveal"
### In-Game Time: Midnight | Flipboard: ROUND 4 — MIDNIGHT

**Scene:** The vote, the confrontation, the confession. This is short and punchy — 15 minutes max.

---

## THE VOTE

The Mentalist addresses the room:

"You've heard the evidence. You've heard the alibis. You've seen the cracks. It's time. Who killed Paloma Rathbone?"

Players vote — verbal, written ballot, or show of hands. The Mentalist tallies.

---

## MAJOR CHARACTERS

### IF GLORIA IS CORRECTLY IDENTIFIED:

### GLORIA REYES — The Confession
- Don't run. Don't deflect. Stand up. Look at Marco.
- Deliver the confession (from memory, paraphrased, or read from a provided card):

"Yes. I killed her.

The shirt was always the plan. Remote. Clean. I gave Irina the compound. She delivered it through the laundry. Paloma would get sick slowly — by morning it would look like a medical event. That was the professional plan.

But at 10:25, I saw her through the conference room door. Stumbling. Grabbing the wall. Still alive. The shirt should have been enough.

But I couldn't sit in that room and wait.

I found her near the laundry. She recognized me. 'Gloria? What are you—'

I injected the paralytic. Ninety seconds.

The shirt was the operation. The injection was me. A mother.

My son Diego was twenty-two. Paloma gave one piece of bad information to the wrong people and called it cost of doing business. She never lost a night of sleep. I've lost every night for two years.

She was going to testify. She was going to bring down everyone in this room — including Marco, who has done nothing except be born into the wrong family.

A mother protects her children. Even the dead ones."

*Looks at Marco.* "I'm sorry you had to see this."

### MARCO REYES
- You're devastated. You knew — on some level you knew. But hearing it is different.
- You don't speak. Or you say one thing: "Mom." That's enough.

### VAL MONTECITO
- Shock. Then anger. Gloria was your family friend. She sat next to you. She comforted Pam. She orchestrated everything.

### ZANE NAKAMURA
- Relief that it wasn't Val. Horror at everything else. You're still in financial ruin, but at least you're not a murder suspect.

### DEX CALLOWAY
- You look at Irina. She followed orders. Gloria's orders. Just like you used to. The cycle continues.

### PAM WORTHINGTON
- Your best friend is a killer. You defended her. You let her comfort you. You're gutted.

### DOUG KAMINSKI
- Your business partner just confessed to murder. Everything is about to unravel. The jokes are over.

### IRINA VOLKOV
- You helped. You know that. The shirt was your hands. You look at Dex — the man who brings you coffee every morning, who doesn't know about Marco. Everything is in the open now.

### SUNNY PATEL
- Your case is destroyed. Your informant is dead. But at least you know who did it. Small comfort.

## MINOR CHARACTERS

### DIEGO REYES (Ghost)
- Watch your mother confess. You're the reason. You don't need to say anything. Your presence is the punctuation.

### ZOYA DAVENPORT
- You saw this coming. Thirty years of knowing Gloria. Nod slowly. "I hoped I was wrong."

### GERALD FINCH / LORRAINE CHU
- You covered for her. That's going to haunt you. Look at each other.

### SASHA KIMURA
- Hold the envelope. The USB drive. Paloma knew this was coming. She tried to leave evidence. It wasn't enough to save her, but it's enough to prove everything.

### ALL OTHERS
- React, discuss, process. The game is over. The conversation after is the real ending.

---

## WRAP-UP

The Mentalist closes:

"Paloma Rathbone was a lot of things — charming, manipulative, brilliant, cruel. She made enemies the way some people make friends: effortlessly and in every direction. But she didn't deserve to die in a laundry basket.

Gloria Reyes is a mother who buried her son and decided someone had to answer for it. Whether that makes her a villain or something more complicated — that's not for me to decide.

Police are here. The investigation is over. But the questions aren't. They never are.

Thank you for playing Dirty Laundry."

**Game over. Applause. Drinks. Discuss.**
