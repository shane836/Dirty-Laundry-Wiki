## ZANE NAKAMURA | The AI/Crypto Hustler

### Era
Late twenties. Startup energy. Made money before understanding how markets work, which is the most dangerous combination in finance.

### Public Persona
Slick, perpetually online crypto guy. Blockchain, AI, "disruption," neural nets, decentralized everything. Expensive streetwear, Rolex, always on phone. Name-drops VCs and seed rounds. Makes everything sound like the future that's already happening. The kind of guy who says "based" unironically and genuinely believes he's thinking bigger than everyone in the room.

### The Real Story
Val's business partner managing the financial architecture of OnlyFans Plus. Crypto wallets, money laundering, digital trails that don't exist, payments that vanish into blockchain. He's competent at it—too competent. He understands how to move seven figures through digital shadows.

But his real secret is devastating: he's desperately, painfully in love with Val.

She sees him as a tool. He sees her as the reason he gets up. Every business meeting is torture—discussing trafficking operations and payment systems while his heart is breaking because she looked at him and didn't see him. She'll never see him romantically. She barely sees him as a human being. He'd burn the whole operation down if she asked. She has no idea.

He's also deeply paranoid. Noticed Sunny photographing documents. Noticed Sunny asking questions that were too specific about account structures. Certain something's off about the "consultant." His paranoia might be justified—or it might be what someone looks like when they're losing their mind over someone who'll never love them back.

### Connection to Richie

Richie spilled margaritas on Zane's hardware crypto wallet during a poolside event. Expensive drink, clumsy moment. But that wallet contained seven figures in trafficking proceeds — **both Val's money AND Gloria's cartel money were stored on the same device.** The spill fried the hardware. The money evaporated into the digital void — not stolen, not traceable, just gone.

Zane is on the hook to both Val AND Gloria for the loss. Val doesn't know yet — she's not angry yet, but she will be. **But the paranoia goes deeper:** Zane suspects the spill was deliberate. He replays the moment constantly. Richie approached too casually. Positioned himself too perfectly. The margarita seemed intentional. Richie was targeting the wallet. Richie was deliberately destroying evidence for his federal testimony. Richie was sabotaging the operation and framing it as accident.

That's not just a financial loss. That's an attack. That's Richie playing 4D chess while Zane was drinking poolside. That's unforgivable.

Business relationship is professional, seamless, completely dysfunctional. Val treats him as a competent tool who handles problems. Zane treats her like she's the sun and he's desperate to get close enough to burn. She has no awareness of his feelings. She'll mention her affair with Richie casually, unaware she's describing his worst nightmare. She'll ask his opinion on her outfit like he's a mirror. He'll analyze her business decisions like he's analyzing his chances of being noticed. It's agony.

Zane doesn't care—he's just moving assets through a compliant courier. But there's irony: Marco is skimming money from one cartel while unwittingly helping Zane launder Only Tans profits.

### Alibi + Versions

**The Alibi**
In Val's private suite doing cocaine with Val, approximately 9:15-11:00 PM. Val confirms the entire timeline. Multiple staff members saw them enter together. Cocaine is real. They came down together around 11 PM. Perfect alibi, completely corroborated.

**Plausible Version**
Val vouches for him—no hesitation, no doubt. Staff saw them go upstairs together. The cocaine is documented and real (tested, photographed). Zane's story matches Val's exactly. They both have motive to lie, but they're alibiing each other, which is actually more suspicious than either lying alone. They seem to have expected to be questioned. Their timeline is smooth, rehearsed.

**Where It Cracks**
Val's suite has back staircase to service area. Ten-minute round trip to laundry room is possible. Did Zane see Val during *every* moment, or just when she entered and exited the suite? They did cocaine together—were they in the same room the whole time, or did they do separate lines in different areas? If you ask follow-up questions, their stories might diverge slightly. Why are two people in a financial crisis doing cocaine instead of actually solving problems? That's denial-level behavior, which suggests they're both panicking. If one of them killed Richie, the alibi holds because the other is lying. Question which one would kill and which one would cover.
