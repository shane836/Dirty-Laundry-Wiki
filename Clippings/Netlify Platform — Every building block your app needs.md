## Every building block. One platform to run it all.

The web development platform built for the way teams ship today: AI agents, serverless, and global delivery, all integrated from day one.

## Build with AI, code, or both.

Agent Runners and platform primitives. Two ways to build on Netlify, one platform that connects them.

### Ship with AI.

Spin up a new project from a prompt, or iterate directly on your live app. Claude Code, Gemini, or Codex handles the code while Netlify handles the rest.

- Every change comes with a Deploy Preview
- No setup or API keys required
- Fits your approval workflows

## The backend your frontend already needs.

Serverless functions, databases, auth, storage, AI model access, and more. All integrated and ready the moment your project starts.

## Iterate more. Break less.

Every push, PR, and prompt gets its own preview URL. Review it, share it, approve it, and deploy when it's ready.

### See every change before it goes live.

Push a branch, open a PR, or run an agent, and Netlify generates a unique preview URL automatically. Share it with your team, test it on any device, and deploy when it's ready.

- Unique preview URL for every branch and PR
- Share with teammates, clients, or stakeholders
- Deploy to production in one click when approved
