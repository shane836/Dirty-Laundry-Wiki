## SECTION A: FIVE MINOR CHARACTERS (OPTIONAL)

These are optional additions—not suspects. Their role: ask expository questions that draw out information about main characters, share colorful gossip and observations, add atmosphere and flavor. The game works without them, but they deepen the investigative experience and provide natural exposition beats.

---

### MINOR 1: FLETCHER "FLETCH" BEAUMONT
**The Conference Photographer**

**Era:** Thirtyish, hipster energy, man-bun, considers himself a "visual storyteller."

**Role:** Hired to document the event professionally. Photographing everything across the three-day conference for the organizers' portfolio.

**Why NOT a Suspect:** Arrived with a broken wrist from a surfing accident the day before. Dominant hand in a cast. Physically incapable of administering poison, moving the body, or committing the crime. Medical records confirm the break and cast application time-stamped 36 hours before the murder. He's adapted — holds his camera in his left hand, shows photos on his phone screen, gestures with his cast for emphasis. The broken wrist hasn't slowed him down, but it has made him more reliant on talking than doing.

**What He Knows:**
- Photographed Val and Richie in a heated argument on the beach the day before the murder. Val crying, Richie pointing, standing uncomfortably close.
- Noticed Marco passing small packages to attendees from the valet stand throughout the day. "I figured mints? But who tips a valet in cash for mints? The amounts were weird."
- Caught Sunny photographing documents sneakily—she didn't know he was nearby. "Weird behavior for a consultant. She was photographing conference materials on someone's table."
- Overheard Doug on the phone saying: "I don't care about the units, I care about the COUNT." Intense for someone discussing porta potties and logistics.

**Questions He Can Ask:**
- *To Val, casually while showing photos:* "Val, I got great shots of you and Richie on the beach yesterday. You two seemed... intense. Want me to delete those for your privacy?"
- *To Marco, friendly banter:* "Marco, buddy, I'm gonna ask a simple question and you're gonna give me a simple answer. What's in those little packages you're handing out?"
- *To Sunny, over a drink:* "Hey Sunny, I saw you photographing documents earlier. Writing a book? Consulting report?"

### MINOR 2: DESTINY RIVERS
**The Influencer**

**Era:** Early twenties, ring light energy, phone battery always at 3%, accent that suggests she learned her influencer vocabulary from TikTok.

**Role:** Instagram and TikTok influencer invited as a "content partner" for the conference. Filming everything, posting Stories, creating behind-the-scenes content.

**Why NOT a Suspect:** Live-streaming on Instagram Stories continuously from 8:30 PM to 11:45 PM. Entire evening is documented with precise timestamps. Hundreds of viewers confirm her location minute-by-minute in real-time. She never left the main party area. Phone geolocation and network data support continuous streaming. Her stream covered the pool and courtyard. Her balcony has only a partial sightline to the service wing — most of the stream shows guests at the party. The one useful detail (Val's back door) was captured because Destiny panned her camera during an Instagram Story about the hotel architecture.

**What She Knows:**
- Filmed Pam getting progressively drunker throughout the evening. Watched the transformation from "fun aunt" energy to something darker and more aggressive. "She went from 'fun aunt' to 'scary aunt' in two hours, bestie."
- Noticed Zane obsessively checking his phone, growing increasingly panicked around 9 PM. "He was literally stress-sweating. Looking at his phone like it was on fire."
- Saw Irina carrying a garment bag to Richie's floor around 7 PM, moving quickly and deliberately. "She basically ran from my camera like she didn't want to be on video."
- Overheard Gloria on the phone in Spanish discussing something about "cleaning up" and "tonight." Gloria's tone was serious—not social. Destiny speaks enough Spanish to know the conversation wasn't casual.

**Questions She Can Ask:**
- *To Pam, with phone in hand:* "Pam, girl, are you okay? I got the footage. Last night you said you were going to 'make Richie pay.' What did you mean by that?"
- *To Zane, concerned influencer energy:* "Zane, what happened at 9 PM? You looked like you lost all your Bitcoin or something. You were freaking out."
- *To Gloria, casually:* "Gloria, hablas español? That phone call sounded pretty serious. Everything okay?"

### MINOR 3: CHIP DAVENPORT
**The Retired Lawyer**

**Era:** Seventyish, grandfatherly demeanor, the kind of retired professional who shows up everywhere uninvited with strong opinions about everything.

**Role:** Retired tax attorney, "wrote the book on offshore trusts." Attends the Tax Optimization Seminar annually for the free drinks and intellectual stimulation. Has been coming for 30 years—basically a fixture.

**Why NOT a Suspect:** Uses a walker for mobility. Hasn't left the main lobby bar area all evening. Six staff members and a security camera confirm he was in a chair by the bar from 7 PM to midnight. Physically cannot reach the laundry area through service corridors.

**What He Knows:**
- Knew Richie back in Moscow in the 1990s. "When he's about to do something stupid, he buys expensive vodka for everyone. Last three days? Buying vodka like he's hosting his own wake. His own wake."
- Recognized Gloria from a Tampa bar association dinner five years ago. "She was taking detailed notes on offshore shell structures. Most spouses tune out completely. She was taking notes like it was her job."
- Saw Dex and Doug in an intense conversation at the bar around 8 PM. "They clearly knew each other from before. Not a first meeting. Odd friendship for a bartender and a porta potty mogul."
- Noticed Richie giving Irina a small package (a book, actually). "They spoke Russian together. It was... tender. Different energy than the rest of his interactions."

**Questions He Can Ask:**
- *To Dex, knowing smile:* "Dex, how do you know The Duke? That didn't look like a first-time meeting. You two go back?"
- *To Gloria, with a hint:* "Gloria, I never forget a face. Tampa Bar Association dinner, 2021. You were taking very detailed notes on Cayman trust structures. Very thorough notes."
- *To Val, gently:* "Val, your father and I go way back. Does Giancarlo know what's actually happening at this hotel? What his daughter's really doing?"

### MINOR 4: REVEREND JAMES "JIMMY" WHITFIELD
**The Conference Chaplain**

**Era:** Early sixties, silver fox energy, dog collar with a Hawaiian shirt, spiritual advisor to the morally bankrupt.

**Role:** Non-denominational chaplain hired by the conference. Runs a confessional booth by the pool where conference attendees drunkenly confess things they absolutely shouldn't. Takes detailed notes for his upcoming "memoir."

**Why NOT a Suspect:** Leading a sunset meditation and spiritual circle with eight witness attendees from 9 PM to 10:30 PM on the resort grounds. Security camera footage confirms he's in the hotel chapel alone from 10:30 PM until midnight, writing his Sunday sermon. Continuous alibi with multiple witnesses and video evidence.

**What He Knows:**
- Richie came to private confession two days before the murder. Distraught, speaking in fragments. "Terrible things," "a woman he'd hurt," "a family he'd destroyed," "people who would kill him." Richie said prayer wasn't going to cut it. "I've gone too far."
  - **HOST NOTE — Path-Dependent Framing:** In all murder paths (Paths 1–9), Jimmy emphasizes fear, not resolve: *"He didn't sound like a man planning to die. He sounded like a man who expected to be killed. There's a difference. One is acceptance. The other is dread. Richie was dreading something."* In Path 10 (Suicide) only, Jimmy emphasizes finality: *"He didn't sound afraid of someone else. He sounded like he'd already decided something. Like he was settling accounts before closing them."*
- Gloria came to confessional at approximately 7:30 PM — before the after-party began. Worried about her son making bad choices. Said: "I would do anything to protect my children. Anything." The way she said "anything" suggested it meant something different than typical parental love. Her eyes suggested willingness.
- Saw Dex leaving Irina's staff quarters early morning (5 AM) with two cups of coffee. "Unlikely friendship, or something more? They made eye contact like they shared something intimate."
- Overheard Zane saying to himself in the courtyard: "She's going to kill me. She's actually going to kill me." He sounded desperate — the way people sound when they realize a financial situation has become existential. The kind of fear that's about losing everything, not about physical danger.

**Questions He Can Ask:**
- *To Val, priestly gravity:* "Richie came to confession very troubled two days ago. He said he'd hurt a woman badly. Val, was that you? What did you do about it?"
- *To Gloria, knowing but kind:* "Gloria, you said you would do 'anything' to protect your children. What does 'anything' look like to you? How far would you actually go?"
- *To Dex, with a gentle smile:* "Dex, I saw you leaving staff quarters at dawn with coffee for two. Who's the lucky lady? Is it Irina?"
