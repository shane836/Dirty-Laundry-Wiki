# Round 4 — "The Reveal"
### Midnight

**Scene:** The vote. The confession. Big reactions. Short and theatrical — 15 minutes max. Gloria carries the weight. Everyone else just gets to react big.

## Your reaction — federal-agent calm

After Gloria confesses:

- **Federal-agent calm.** This is your case now.
- Step toward Gloria. Don't grab her — she's not running. Deliver the line: *"Mrs. Reyes. You'll come with me."*

## Backup speech — "Let me read you the room"

> **ONLY DELIVER IF the vote does not land on Gloria.** The host runs the vote tally and will signal you. If players vote correctly, skip this entirely — Gloria stands and confesses on her own. If players miss it (wrong vote, split vote, or chicken out), the host will turn to you. Then stand and deliver the speech below. You're the federal agent — you have the authority to call it.

Stand. Take the room. Deliver evenly, with the weight of the badge:

> *"Let me read you the room. Mrs. Reyes? Stand up, please.*
>
> *Five browns on this table. A burnt note in Mrs. Volkov's apron. A Sharpie disguised as an insulin pen. A son she buried two years ago. A son she's protecting tonight. A watch checked four times in ten minutes before she stepped out for what was definitely not a restroom break.*
>
> *Ladies and gentlemen — you missed it. The killer is Gloria Reyes."*

Gloria will stand. The host will run the confession from there. You return to federal-agent calm.
