# Round 1 — "The After-Party"
### 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM

**Scene:** The after-party in the courtyard. Steel drum music, glowing pool, cocktails. Paloma is supposed to give a toast at 9 PM. She doesn't show. By the end of this round, the body is found and the gates are shut.

- You're the hostess. Welcome everyone to YOUR hotel. Be glamorous, confident, in control. Champagne in hand.
- Mention Zane casually — you two have been "working on something" together. Don't say what.
- If anyone asks about Paloma, act annoyed she's late. "Typical Paloma. Always has to make an entrance." Call her room — no answer.
- When the body is found, you go pale. Grip your drink tightly. Say nothing.

---

# Round 2 — "Everyone's a Suspect"
### 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM

**Scene:** Everyone's inside. The party is over. The host opens, then turns the players loose to talk to each other. Move, pair off, trade alibis and secrets in conversation. **Yes-and rule:** if someone asks you something not on your card, play along; make it up consistent with your character.

- **Your alibi:** "I was in my suite from about 9:15. Zane was with me. We were... relaxing." Hesitate on "relaxing."
- **Share in conversation:**
  - If pressed on when you last saw Paloma, be vague. "This morning? Maybe last night?" You're lying about something — but maybe not about murder.
  - If Fiona shows you the beach photos, get defensive: "Where are those photos? Delete them."
  - You own the only override card. Don't volunteer that. If asked directly, deflect: "It's in my safe."
- **Seek out:** Zane (lock in your story), Fiona (find out what she photographed), Sasha (find out what Paloma left behind).
- Stay near Zane physically. Eye contact with him every time someone asks the wrong question.

## When Sunny stands up (federal stakes — ~minute 20)

When Sunny drops the corporate persona, opens his padfolio, and produces the federal badge + indictment list — announcing he's IRS-Interpol and Paloma was his informant set to testify Monday:

- **Drop your champagne glass.** (Shatters or doesn't — actor's choice.) Big public reaction. The room registers it.
- After the drop, freeze. Don't speak. Stay close to Zane. Your hostess composure has just cracked.
- When Sasha steps forward and hands Sunny the sealed envelope — watch them. Don't intervene.

# Round 3 — "The Investigation"
### 11:00 PM – Midnight

**Scene:** Hard evidence. Investigation by conversation. Press the alibis. Look at the body. Open bags. Two visual climaxes anchor the round (the five browns, then Val cleared / Marco's eyewitness) — driven by characters in the room, not the host.

- **The override card is your problem.** When pressed, deny initially: "I didn't go to her floor. Someone else must have used it." Hold the lie until Pepe's work order surfaces.
- **Share in conversation:**
  - When Zane breaks ("She left for ice. Twenty minutes."), don't fight it. Stop talking. Let it sit.
  - When Pepe finally hands over the maintenance work order — exhale. "I was on Floor 3. Leaking unit. 10:05. The override was a maintenance call. Ask Pepe."
- **Seek out:** Zane (control the damage), Pepe (find out if he has the work order — he does).
- Late in the round, when Gloria's guilt emerges, you're visibly relieved. Then angry. Then scared — Sunny's reveal means your operation is exposed too.

## When Pepe clears you (late round)

When Pepe walks the maintenance work order to the center of the room and announces: "Floor 3. Leaking unit. The override card was a maintenance call. Val Montecito is cleared."

- **Exhale for the first time all round.** Make it visible. Shoulders drop. Eyes close for a beat.
- That exhale is your moment. The room needs to see your relief land before Marco speaks.

# Round 4 — "The Reveal"
### Midnight

**Scene:** The vote. The confession. Big reactions. Short and theatrical — 15 minutes max. Gloria carries the weight. Everyone else just reacts big.

You don't have lines you have to deliver. You have **big, theatrical reactions.** Lean in. Soap opera. Telenovela. The bigger the better. If a line below feels right, say it. Otherwise just react big and stay in character.

- **Shock.** Then anger. Then a thousand-yard stare.
- *"She sat next to me. She comforted Pam. She orchestrated everything."*
- Drop your champagne glass for real this time, if it isn't already broken.
