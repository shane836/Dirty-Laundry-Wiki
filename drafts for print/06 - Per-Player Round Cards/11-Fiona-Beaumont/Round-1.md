# Round 1 — "The After-Party"
### 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM

**Scene:** The after-party in the courtyard. Steel drum music, glowing pool, cocktails. Paloma is supposed to give a toast at 9 PM. She doesn't show. By the end of this round, the body is found and the gates are shut.

- You're the photographer. Snap photos of everything. Show people shots on your phone. Be nosy and friendly.
- Take note of who's talking to whom. You're documenting the party.
- **Specifically photograph Marco when he opens his wallet to look at the photo inside. Make a small show of it: "Sorry, candid shot, ignore me." You won't show this picture until Round 3 — but the host has already pre-loaded a close-up on your phone for that moment.**
