# Round 2 — "Everyone's a Suspect"
### 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM

**Scene:** Everyone's inside. The party is over. The host opens, then turns the players loose to talk to each other. Move, pair off, trade alibis and secrets in conversation. **Yes-and rule:** if someone asks you something not on your card, play along; make it up consistent with your character.

- **Share in conversation:**
  - **The propped service door:** *"It's been wedged since 6 PM. Fire-code violation. Somebody wanted that door open on purpose."*
  - **Missing chemical supplies:** *"I filed a missing-supplies report two days ago. Cleaning compounds out of the maintenance closet. Rosa never followed up."*
  - **Marco's car move:** Corroborate when his keycard at 10:07 comes up. *"I smelled his cigarette behind the shed at ten. He moved the Range Rover."*
  - **The broken spray bottle:** Hold this in reserve until your moment to drive (below).
- **Hold back the maintenance work order — that's a Round 3 reveal.**
- **Seek out:** Rosa (hand off your missing-supplies report), Marco (he owes you).

- Then nod to her. **Pam's cleared.** Let her toast the room with her fishbowl.
