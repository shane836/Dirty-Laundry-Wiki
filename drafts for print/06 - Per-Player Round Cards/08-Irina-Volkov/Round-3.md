# Round 3 — "The Investigation"
### 11:00 PM – Midnight

**Scene:** Hard evidence. Investigation by conversation. Press the alibis. Look at the body. Open bags. Two visual climaxes anchor the round (the five browns, then Val cleared / Marco's eyewitness moment) — these are run by characters in the room, not by the host.

## Your moment to drive — the break (mid-round, in conversation)

This is your big moment and it is yours alone. It does not happen on a host cue — it lands when players push you on the garment bag, the master key, or the 7 PM delivery. When the pressure is on you, **break.**

- **Drop the broken English mid-conversation.** Switch to perfect English. Let the room hear the change.
- Reach into your apron, pull out the burnt-edge note, and hand it over to whoever is closest (or to Sunny if he's near).
- Deliver the line, evenly and clearly: *"Gloria gave me the compound. Gloria ordered the shirt. I delivered it. I tried to burn the note. I didn't try hard enough."*

## If asked, three answers

- **Why:** "She pays for my mother's medical care. My daughter's school. Without her, they have nothing. I had no choice."
- **About moving the body:** "I found her in the corridor. I knew. I moved her. It was instinct."
- **About the coffee tin:** "Pepe has it. It is on my cart. It is not coffee."

## When Sunny lines up the five browns

Later in the round, Sunny (the federal agent) will drive a visual climax — he'll demand Gloria empty her tote, line up the Sharpie next to the collar next to the handkerchief next to Gloria's stained cuff next to **the powder in your coffee tin** — five matching browns. When he holds up the powder from your tin:

- **Confirm with a nod.** That's all. You don't have to speak. The nod tells the room he's right.

## Seek out

- Pepe (he has the tin and the cotton ball — when he raises them, you confirm).
- Whoever is closest when you break — hand them the burnt note.
