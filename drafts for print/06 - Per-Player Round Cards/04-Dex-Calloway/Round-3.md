## Your moment to drive — the thermos contrast (Five Browns climax)

This is the visual climax of the night. After Sunny crosses to Gloria, demands her bag, peels back the masking tape on the Sharpie ("you are not diabetic. And this is a Sharpie."), and lines up the Sharpie + collar + handkerchief + Gloria's stained cuff + the powder from Irina's coffee tin — five matching browns on the table — Sunny will turn to the bar.

- **Sunny calls: "Mr. Calloway. The thermos."**
- Pull the thermos out from under the bar. Unscrew the lid. **Steam rises.** Pour a cup on the bartop — real coffee, different shade.
- Deliver: *"It's coffee. Smell it. It's been coffee for six months."*
- Sunny finishes the contrast: "*That* is coffee. *This* is the same brown as the stain on Ms. Rathbone's collar." Let him close it. Step back behind the bar.
