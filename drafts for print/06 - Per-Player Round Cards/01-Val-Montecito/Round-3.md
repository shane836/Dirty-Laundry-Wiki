# Round 3 — "The Investigation"
### 11:00 PM – Midnight

**Scene:** Hard evidence. Investigation by conversation. Press the alibis. Look at the body. Open bags. Two visual climaxes anchor the round (the five browns, then Val cleared / Marco's eyewitness) — driven by characters in the room, not the host.

- **The override card is your problem.** When pressed, deny initially: "I didn't go to her floor. Someone else must have used it." Hold the lie until Pepe's work order surfaces.
- **Share in conversation:**
  - When Zane breaks ("She left for ice. Twenty minutes."), don't fight it. Stop talking. Let it sit.
  - When Pepe finally hands over the maintenance work order — exhale. "I was on Floor 3. Leaking unit. 10:05. The override was a maintenance call. Ask Pepe."
- **Seek out:** Zane (control the damage), Pepe (find out if he has the work order — he does).
- Late in the round, when Gloria's guilt emerges, you're visibly relieved. Then angry. Then scared — Sunny's reveal means your operation is exposed too.

## When Pepe clears you (late round)

When Pepe walks the maintenance work order to the center of the room and announces: "Floor 3. Leaking unit. The override card was a maintenance call. Val Montecito is cleared."

- **Exhale for the first time all round.** Make it visible. Shoulders drop. Eyes close for a beat.
- That exhale is your moment. The room needs to see your relief land before Marco speaks.
