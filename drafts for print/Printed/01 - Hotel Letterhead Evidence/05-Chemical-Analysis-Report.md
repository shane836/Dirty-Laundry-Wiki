> **FINDINGS — Exhibit CIGL-488-A (collar residue):**
>
> Sample is a plant-derived alkaloid compound, slow-acting on dermal contact. Identification consistent with a cardio-active glycoside in a refined particulate carrier. The compound is **deep brown** in its concentrated form and is routinely cut with, or disguised within, **roasted coffee or cocoa preparations** — the color and aroma of either readily mask the agent at typical dosing.
>
> Onset via skin contact: gradual, 1–3 hours. Symptoms: progressive weakness, blurred vision, cardiac irregularity. Dermal exposure of the magnitude observed on Exhibit A is consistent with significant systemic absorption over an evening's wear, but is **not, in isolation, acutely lethal** within that window.
