# Incident Report — Print Spec

**Use:** Establishes Marco's grudge against Paloma — she had her sports car scratched, blamed the valet, and humiliated Marco at the front of the property. Marco signed the report in anger.

**Layout notes**
- Hotel letterhead header.
- Form-style layout with labeled fields and ruled lines (mimics a fillable hotel incident report).
- "Comments" box near the bottom is the focal point — leave it large enough that the post-print Sharpie scratch reads from across a table.
- **MANUAL STEP AFTER PRINT:** with a heavy black ballpoint or fine Sharpie, scribble out the entire Comments box in tight diagonal hatching (Marco redacted his own words in a fit). Leave Marco's signature visible *underneath* / next to the scratch.
