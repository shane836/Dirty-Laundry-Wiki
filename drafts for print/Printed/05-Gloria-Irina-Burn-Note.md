# "Burn This" Note — Matched Pair — Print Spec

**Use:** The smoking-gun pair. Identical handwritten notes — Gloria gives one to Irina at 7:00 PM (laundry handoff), keeps the second clean copy in her own tote in case anyone searches her ("just a shopping note, see?"). Irina tries to burn hers and fails — only a corner scorches before she's interrupted. When the room searches both women in Round 3, the matched pair is the kill shot: same hand, same words, one half-burned.

**Layout notes**
- Use a **handwritten font**: **Caveat**, **Homemade Apple**, or **Shadows Into Light**. NOT Comic Sans. NOT a typewriter font. Should look like a quick note jotted in a hurry.
- Slight ink-pressure variation if the font supports it. Black or very dark blue "ballpoint" feel.
- Small piece of paper, ~3.5" x 2.5" (about the size of a notepad page).
- Optional: faint horizontal blue rule lines (notebook paper). Optional: torn top edge as if pulled from a spiral pad.
- **PRINT TWO IDENTICAL COPIES.**
- **POST-PRINT — copy #2 only:** physically scorch ONE corner with a lighter, candle, or kitchen torch. 5–8 seconds. Want a blackened, slightly curled, sooty edge. **Do not burn through the paper, do not burn the text.** A thin black margin ~1/4" deep into one corner is the target. Practice on a scrap first.
- The two copies should otherwise be indistinguishable — same hand, same ink, same paper.
