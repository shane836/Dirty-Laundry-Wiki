# Valet Stand Sign-In / Sign-Out Sheet — Print Spec

**Use:** Marco's working valet log. He carries it on a clipboard at the valet stand all evening. When his alibi is questioned in Round 2/3, he produces this. Most of the night reads as a tight, professional log in his initials ("MR"). The 9:58 PM – 10:13 PM stretch is conspicuously **blank** — that's the gap when Marco moved Paloma's car to cover for Val/Zane and was off-stand for ~15 minutes. Looks suspicious. Marco isn't the killer; the gap is real but innocent.

**Layout notes**
- Top: Heathens Hollow Hotel letterhead (smaller than the front-desk reports). Sub-stamp: "VALET STAND · DAILY TICKET LOG."
- Body is a hand-fillable grid: TIME IN · TIME OUT · GUEST / PARTY · VEHICLE · TAG # · STALL · INITIALS.
- Use a typewriter/monospaced font for the form rules and headers (Courier / Special Elite). Body entries should look hand-printed — use a casual handwriting font like **Caveat** or **Architects Daughter**.
- Most rows should be in one consistent hand ("MR" — Marco Reyes). A few earlier rows (7:00 – 9:30 PM) should look like a *slightly different hand* and use initials "JT" — the dinner-shift valet who clocked out at 9:30. This is what makes the 9:58–10:13 gap read cleanly: it's only Marco's solo stretch.
- The 9:58–10:13 PM window: leave the row LITERALLY BLANK between the 9:55 PM return and the 10:14 PM departure. No tag, no initials, just empty form ruling. That blank is the prop.
- Hand-fill the last two rows (post-11 PM) and the 9:55 PM "return" row in blue ballpoint after printing — slight ink-pressure variation.
- Optional: faint coffee ring upper-right corner. Slightly bent corner from clipboard wear.
