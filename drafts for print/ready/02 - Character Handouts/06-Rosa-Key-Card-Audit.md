# Night Manager's Daily Key Audit — Print Spec

**Use:** Rosa's working clipboard document. She carries it all night; in Round 2 she hands it to the Mentalist during the "Key Card Logs" beat to establish: (1) only four master keys exist plus one owner override, (2) her physical-key audit is **off by one** because Pepe never returned his at shift-end, and (3) the 22:07 PM master-key swipe in the service area cannot be assigned to a specific key. Narrows the suspect pool while keeping the killer ambiguous between the master-key holders and anyone who could have lifted Pepe's key from his toolbox.

**Layout notes**
- Heathens Hollow letterhead at top, smaller and more "internal" than the guest-facing reports — sub-stamp: "FRONT OFFICE · KEY CONTROL — NIGHT MANAGER COPY."
- Use a **clean sans-serif** for form headers (Helvetica Neue / Arial Narrow / Inter) and **Courier / Special Elite** for all roster data and times.
- Three blocks stacked top-to-bottom on a single page:
  1. **Master Key Issuance Roster** — 4 numbered key fobs with issuance time, holder, return time, status, signature.
  2. **Owner-Class Override Card** — single line, separate stamped block, "OWNER-CLASS — RESTRICTED" red corner.
  3. **Tonight's Reconciliation Notes** — Rosa's hand-written-style observations.
- The "RETURNED" column for keys 1–3 is filled. **Key 4 (P. Stevens, MK-04) — RETURNED column is blank.** Status reads **"OUTSTANDING — see Note 2."** This is the prop's whole point.
- Bottom right: pre-printed reconciliation summary box reading: "ISSUED: 4 · RETURNED (logged): 3 · OUTSTANDING: 1 · DISCREPANCY: AUDIT OFF BY ONE."
- Hand-fill the bottom signature line ("R. Martinez, Night Mgr.") in blue ballpoint and date with the event date. The "Returned" times for keys 1–3 should also be hand-filled blue ballpoint to read as live.
- Optional: red rubber-stamp "INTERNAL — DO NOT DISTRIBUTE" angled across the lower margin.
