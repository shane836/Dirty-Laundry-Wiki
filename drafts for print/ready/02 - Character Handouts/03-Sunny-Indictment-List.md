# Federal Indictment List — Print Spec

**Use:** Sunny's reveal prop. He stands at the end of Round 2 and drops "Stratton Consulting" — pulls this from a folder and reads names aloud. Has to be **scannable in 5 seconds** so guests can clock their own character. Drives the R3 panic: the room understands the cocktail party is also a federal sting.

**Layout notes**
- Serif body font (**Times New Roman** or Equity) — looks like a real federal court filing.
- Top center: "UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT" / "SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF NEW YORK" / "FOLEY SQUARE."
- Case caption block left-justified: "UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, v. RATHBONE et al." with case number.
- Title: "INDICTMENT" centered, all-caps, bold, with subtitle "(Sealed — Pending Grand Jury Return)."
- Numbered defendant list — Roman numerals or arabic — name in **bold caps**, then count(s) in italics underneath.
- Footer: AUSA signature line, court seal placeholder, "FILED UNDER SEAL — 18 U.S.C. § 3500" notation.
- Optional red diagonal "SEALED" watermark across the page at 15% opacity.
