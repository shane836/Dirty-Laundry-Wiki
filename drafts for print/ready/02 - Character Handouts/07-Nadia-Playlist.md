# DJ Booth Setlist & Continuous-Play Log — Print Spec

**Use:** Nadia's working cue-sheet. She keeps it taped to the lip of the DJ booth all night so she can read it without looking down. When her location is questioned in Round 2 ("Were you really at the booth all night?"), she points to this and to the venue's logged playback timestamps. Every track has a printed cue-time. The list is unbroken from 19:00 to 00:30. **Visual proof that the booth was actively driven across the entire kill window** — Nadia couldn't have been anywhere else without dead-air, which dozens of guests would have noticed.

**Layout notes**
- Header: bold sans-serif "DJ NADIA OSEI" with a small "HEATHENS HOLLOW · POOLSIDE BOOTH" sub-header.
- Use a clean **sans-serif** (Helvetica / Inter / Avenir) for headers, **monospaced** (Courier / Roboto Mono) for the track grid so the time column lines up.
- Body is a four-column grid: CUE TIME · TRACK · ARTIST · BPM (or LEN). Optional fifth column: NOTES.
- Group into named sets ("Arrivals · Cocktail Hour" → "Sunset" → "Peak" → "After-Hours" → "Body-Found · Houselights") with a thin horizontal rule between each set. The final set's tracks should be **struck through with a single blue line** — these never played. The booth was cut at 22:31 when the body was discovered.
- Bottom of the page: a small "PLAYBACK SOURCE LOG" stamp confirming continuous output (no gap > 6 sec between tracks) for system audit. Mirrors the venue's automated logging — a second, independent witness to her location.
- Hand-mark, after printing:
  - Circle 2-3 tracks in blue ballpoint with "GUEST REQ — D. Kaminski" or similar.
  - Strike one track through with "skipped — too slow."
  - Tiny initials "N.O." next to a couple of cue times she manually nudged.
  - Coffee/condensation ring lower-right corner.
- All track titles and artist names are **fictional**. Tropical / island / dance vibe consistent with the Caribbean party theme. No real songs.
