# Doug's Pittsburgh Souvenir Receipt — Print Spec

**Use:** Doug's "proof" he was in Pittsburgh, not Grand Cayman, on the night of the murder. He pulls this out any time anyone questions his alibi. The whole thing is a bad fake — printed in **Comic Sans**, dated for **TOMORROW** (one day AFTER the party). Doug doesn't notice. The audience does. Foreshadows the R3 reveal that Doug is Gloria's money-laundering shell.

**Layout notes**
- **FONT IS COMIC SANS** for the entire receipt. This is non-negotiable — that is the joke. (If the print shop balks, Comic Sans MS or any equivalent rounded handwriting-imitation font.)
- Narrow column, register-tape feel. ~3.25" wide. Center-aligned header.
- Top: shop name big, address smaller, fake phone number, fake "thank you for visiting Pittsburgh!" tagline.
- Itemized list, dot-leader style price column on the right.
- **Date prints as TOMORROW** — i.e. `[event date + 1 day]`. Spell out the date so the host can hand-write the actual day-of value at production time. Time printed: 3:47 PM.
- Tax line: "PA Sales Tax 6%" (Pittsburgh-correct enough to almost pass).
- Footer: a tacky "Y'INZ COME BACK NOW!" or "Go Stillers!" line.
- Slightly crumple before handing to Doug.
