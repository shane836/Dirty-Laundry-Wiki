# Hotel Staff ID Badges — Print Spec

**Use:** A small subset of characters work AT the hotel and wear a SECOND credential — a real-looking employee ID — in addition to their lanyard nametag. The badge sells "this person is staff" on first glance. Pepe's hire date (2009) is the longest of the five — visual cue that he knows where every body is buried. Marco's hire date (2025) is the newest — visual cue that he's still proving himself.

**Layout notes**
- Brand palette: deep teal + cream + sand/coral accent (consistent with all signage).
- Portrait orientation, 2.125x3.375".
- Top strip (deep teal): "I.D." left-aligned in heavy sans-serif white caps, Heathens Hollow logo right-aligned.
- Photo placeholder rectangle centered upper third — print "[ photo ]" placeholder or leave a 1x1.25" cream block for a stand-in photo.
- Below photo: NAME (heavy sans-serif, deep teal, all-caps), DEPARTMENT (lighter sans-serif, sand/coral).
- Data block (small caps, monospaced): EMPLOYEE #, HIRE DATE, EXPIRES.
- Bottom: thin black barcode-style strip (decorative — does not need to scan), with a faux serial under it.
- 5 mil gloss or matte laminate. Hole-punch top-center 1/4" hole. Breakaway lanyard required (real safety detail — also reads as authentic).
- Print 8-up on letter-size for shop efficiency; trim and laminate.
