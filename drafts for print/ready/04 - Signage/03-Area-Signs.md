# Area Signs — Print Spec

**Use:** Four area signs ground the venue as a working hotel. The LAUNDRY ROOM sign is visual setup for the body-discovery beat (the joke lands when the body shows up where the sign says "Staff Only"). SERVICE CORRIDOR labels the space where the murder actually happens — the room reads it as off-limits early, then revisits it once the killer is revealed.

**Layout notes**
- Brand palette: deep teal + cream + sand/coral accent (consistent with nametags and IDs).
- Landscape orientation — wider read distance, easier to wall-mount above doorframes.
- Full-bleed 1" deep-teal border. Cream interior field.
- Hotel logo small, top-left corner inside border (deep teal monogram, ~1" tall).
- AREA NAME centered, MASSIVE sans-serif heavy (e.g., Inter Black or Futura Bold), all-caps, deep teal — readable from 25+ ft.
- Subtitle below in lighter weight sans-serif, sand/coral accent color, ~1/3 the size of the main name.
- Optional thin sand/coral accent rule between name and subtitle.
- Heavy cardstock 80–100 lb so the signs don't curl when taped up. Matte finish to kill glare under house lights.
- Mount with painter's tape or removable mounting putty so they survive the night without damaging walls.
