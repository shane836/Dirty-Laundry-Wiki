# Round Flipboard Cards — Print Spec

**Use:** Four oversize cards sit on an easel (or hang from binder rings) at the front of the room. The host physically flips to the next card at each round transition — gives ceremony to the time jump and lets every guest see exactly what round it is and what the in-fiction time is. Big enough to read across the party.

**Layout notes**
- Brand palette: deep teal + cream + sand/coral accent (consistent with all signage).
- Landscape 11x17". One round per card. Print 4 cards total.
- Cream background with full-bleed 1.25" deep-teal border (matches area signs as a visual family).
- Hotel logo small, top-right corner (~1" tall, deep teal).
- ROUND number MASSIVE — top-center, sans-serif heavy (Inter Black / Futura Bold), all-caps, deep teal. This is the dominant element on the card.
- TIME below the round number, slightly smaller, sand/coral accent color, monospaced or condensed sans-serif (reads like a clock).
- SUBTITLE smaller still, centered below the time, lighter weight sans-serif, deep teal or warm gray.
- 100 lb cover cardstock so the cards stand up on an easel and flip without flopping.
- Two-hole punch top-center (2.75" centers) for standard binder rings — alternative: clip to a flipchart easel and flip back over the top.
- Stack in order R1 → R2 → R3 → R4. Host flips at each transition for ceremony.
