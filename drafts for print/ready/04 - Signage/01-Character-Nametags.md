# Character Nametags — Print Spec

**Use:** Every player wears a Heathens Hollow Hotel branded nametag on a lanyard for the entire event. Grounds the room as a Caymans tax-optimization conference and gives guests an easy reference for who's who. Sunny Patel and Doug Kaminski's tags lean into their COVER identities only — do NOT spoil federal-agent or launderer reveals (those land in R2/R3). Diego's nametag reads "IN MEMORIAM" because the character is the ghost; Esther wears it to identify her role to the room without breaking the conceit. Fortunado's tag (the host, Shane) is more theatrical — gold lettering or alternate stock acceptable.

**Layout notes**
- Brand palette: deep teal + cream + sand/coral accent. Tag body cream so handwriting and printed text read cleanly.
- Hotel logo top-left (small, deep teal). Hairline sand/coral accent rule under the logo bar.
- Character NAME centered, sans-serif heavy (e.g., Inter Black, Futura Bold, or Trade Gothic Bold) — large, all-caps, deep teal.
- TITLE underneath, lighter weight sans-serif, slightly smaller, sand/coral or warm gray.
- Bottom-edge subline (very small caps, light gray): "Tax Optimization Seminar — April [event year]"
- Single-sided. Slot punch top-center (3/8" x 1/16" slot) for standard lanyard clip; alternate: round pin hole top-right if pin-back.
- Print 8-up or 10-up on a single 8.5x11 sheet for shop efficiency; cut to 3.5x2.25".
- Fortunado's tag: same template but swap deep teal for matte gold ink (or foil) on the NAME line, and use a slightly darker cream stock if available — visually flags him as the host/master of ceremonies.
