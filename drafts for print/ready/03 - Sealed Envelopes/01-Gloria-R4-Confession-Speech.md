# Gloria's R4 Confession — Print Spec

**Use:** The climax of the party. After the Mentalist names Gloria in R4, Gloria stands, breaks the seal, and reads aloud. This is the load-bearing emotional moment — the room needs to feel Diego, not just hear a confession. Gloria is a quiet working-class mother, not a villain monologuing. Keep it small.

**Layout notes**
- Set in a serif body face (Garamond, Sabon) at ~13pt. Generous line spacing — the player will be reading under pressure.
- Title block at top, bold, centered. Then a thin horizontal rule.
- *Stage directions in italics, in line with the text.* Do not over-direct.
- Print single-sided. Fold in thirds, letter-style, into the #10 envelope.
- On the envelope front, hand-letter the warning in black marker (do not print it — handwriting reads more "personal").
- Spoken target: 60–90 seconds. ~240 words.

## COPY TO PRINT

> **FOR GLORIA REYES — TO BE READ ALOUD AT ROUND 4**
>
> ---
>
> *(Stand. Take your time. You are not performing — you are finally speaking.)*
>
> Yes. I did this. All of it.
>
> My son Diego was twenty-six. He was an electrician. He used to whistle the same three notes coming up the stairs — I always knew it was him before the key turned. Paloma's people, her offshore world, her *clever* paperwork — they took a small business he had borrowed against, and they fed it into something he could not understand. He lost the shop. He lost the apartment. He lost the way he stood. One morning he did not come up the stairs.
>
> I made him *pollo guisado* the Sunday before. He had two plates.
>
> *(A pause. Do not rush.)*
>
> I have been waiting six years. I got close to Irina — her family was hurt by the same people, in a different country, in the same way. She did the slow part. The shirt, at seven o'clock, through garment service. I did the fast part. The corridor, at ten twenty-five. The tin in my apron said "Café Cubano." It did not.
>
> I am not sorry. I traded my freedom for his peace. That was the trade. I made it with open eyes.
>
> *(Turn slightly toward Marco. Do not ask anything of him.)*
>
> Marco. *Mijo.* I made you an orphan twice tonight. I know that. I know.
>
> *(Sit down. Hand the page to the Mentalist.)*
