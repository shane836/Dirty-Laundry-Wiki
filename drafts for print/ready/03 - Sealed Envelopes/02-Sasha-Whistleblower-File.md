# Whistleblower File — Print Spec

**Use:** The chain-locking document. In R2, Sasha (Paloma's PA) hands Sunny the sealed envelope. In R3, Sunny breaks the seal, slides out the USB and the page, and reads the findings aloud. This is where the room learns Gloria is the named beneficial owner of the trust — Paloma was about to cooperate against her own laundering network, and Gloria's name was on the page.

**Layout notes**
- Set in a clean serif (Times, Charter) at 11pt. Single column. Tight, dry, lawyerly.
- Title block centered, bold, all caps. Subtitle in smaller italics ("Prepared by P. Rathbone for federal cooperation — DRAFT").
- A small "DRAFT — ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT" stamp top-right (gray, low opacity, slightly crooked).
- Numbered findings, no bullets. Indent the numbers; let the body breathe.
- Sign-off bottom-right: "— P. Rathbone" in serif italic, with the date placeholder.
- Tuck the USB into the same envelope. The flatness of the prose is the menace.
