# Dirty Laundry v3 — Revision Plan

**Status:** APPROVED — Executing edits
**Date:** May 4, 2026
**Source material:** Murder Mystery v2 main folder, notes from Shane, Stephen Jackson, and Dina

---

## Big Picture

The v2 draft is rich but overcomplicated. The v3 revision simplifies everything down to a playable party game with one killer path (Gloria), concise character dossiers, round cards that tell players exactly what to say/do, and a clear props list. We're also adding five new minor characters and making specific character changes per the notes.

**Simplification principle:** Strip out exact dollar amounts, debt ledgers, and granular financial details. Players don't need to track who owes whom $43K vs $200K vs $80K. Replace with plain-language stakes: "a massive debt," "enough money to disappear," "laundering serious cash." The drama is in the relationships and motives, not the accounting.

**Core decisions locked in:**
- **Gloria is the killer.** One path only. No branching paths.
- **Paloma Rathbone is female.** Love interests (Val, Pam) are now queer/bisexual women. All pronouns updated.
- **3 secrets per character** (down from 5-8). Every character stays plausible as a suspect through Round 3.
- **4 rounds** with printed round cards per character.
- **Diego is a playable ghost character** — assigned to a guest, reveals backstory over rounds.
- **Paloma (the victim) is played by Dina**, who will be busy cooking. Lean into the comedy. Body proxy: stuffed clothes in a laundry basket.
- **Stephen's dual-method fix is approved.** The poisoned shirt was cold cartel logic (the plan). The personal injection was a mother who needed to look Paloma in the eye (for Diego). Gloria's confession acknowledges: "The shirt should have been enough. But I needed to look her in the eye."

## 1. MASTER STORYLINE DOCUMENT

A single clean narrative document laying out the Gloria-is-the-killer plot, organized by round.

### Key revisions

**Paloma is female.** This changes:
- Val had an affair with Paloma → same-sex relationship. Val is bisexual.
- Pam had a complicated relationship with Paloma → Pam is bisexual or it was romantic tension/obsession.
- "Wife in Moscow" → "husband in Moscow" or simply "estranged spouse back in Russia."
- The "handsy drunk" characterization stays — Paloma is a handsy drunk who gets touchy and grabby after a few drinks. She uses intimacy as currency and alcohol as an excuse.
- Paloma's Russian heritage stays. She's a Russian-born woman who built a career in offshore tax law through charisma and ruthlessness.

**Host role → The Detective (Shane)**
Rosa was the hotel night manager who locked things down and ran the investigation. That role is now split:
- **The Detective** (Host/Shane): You are a private investigator hired by the hotel's insurance company. You arrived at the party as a guest (blending in), but when the body drops, you reveal yourself and take charge. Clipboard, authority, dramatic flair. You shut the gates, run the alibi round, deliver evidence beats, and control pacing. This is the classic murder mystery host role — you're Poirot with a Hawaiian shirt.
- Why this works: it's fun, it's genre-appropriate, it gives you a reason to hold a clipboard, interrogate everyone, and make dramatic announcements. Way more engaging than "hotel night manager."

**Rosa Martinez stays as Night Manager (female)**
Rosa remains the night manager NPC. Since the Host is now the Detective (named Remy Marchand), Rosa's role is a supporting NPC who:
- Provides the master key log data when the Detective asks for it
- Confirms the timeline details (who arrived when, key card swipes)
- Can be played by the Host as a secondary voice, or assigned to a guest as a small speaking role
- Her key function: she's the one who hands the Detective the evidence (key card logs, incident reports, security footage notes)

**The Detective → Remy Marchand (Shane)**
Shane's host character is named Remy Marchand — a private investigator hired by the hotel's insurance company.

**Venue mapping — Brian's property:**
- **Main structure** (side entrance + front entrance) = the hotel lobby, bar area, and conference room. The side entrance = the "service stairwell" that Gloria uses to slip out. Indoor scenes (alibis, accusations, evidence reveals) happen here.
- **Side structure** (one entrance) = the "service area" including the laundry room where the body is found. The body proxy lives here.
- **Shared yard** = the pool/courtyard area where the after-party takes place. Round 1 mingle happens outdoors. The Detective can dramatically move everyone inside (main structure) after the body is discovered.
- **Host Guide** will include a venue map note explaining how to label these areas with printed signs ("LOBBY," "LAUNDRY ROOM," "SERVICE CORRIDOR," etc.)

**Round timing and alibi timestamps:**
The four rounds map to in-game time. Each round's timestamp should be displayed on a cardboard flipboard and announced by the Detective. The timestamps matter because alibis reference specific times:

| Round | In-Game Time | Real Duration | Key Alibi Windows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1 "The After-Party" | 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM | ~30 min | Paloma orders vodka at 7 PM. Toast expected at 9 PM. Body found ~10:30 PM |
| Round 2 "Everyone's a Suspect" | 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM | ~30 min | Val+Zane in suite from 9:15. Marco's key swipe at 10:07. Gloria in conference room. Pam blacks out ~9:45 |
| Round 3 "The Investigation" | 11:00 PM – Midnight | ~45 min | Gloria left conference room at 10:25. Doug arrived via service entrance. Override card swiped at 10:05 |
| Round 4 "The Reveal" | Midnight | ~15 min | Vote, confession, wrap |

The flipboard timestamps help players track alibi claims against the timeline. The Detective should reference the clock when pressing characters: "So you say you were in your suite at 9:15, but the override card was swiped at 10:05..."

**Zane and Val staying suspect through Round 3:**
- Round 2: Their mutual alibi holds but feels rehearsed (keep as-is).
- Round 3: Owner's override card confirmed as Val's, swiped at 10:05 PM on Paloma's floor. This makes Val look guilty through most of Round 3. A witness saw "someone" on the back staircase around 10 PM — could be Val or Zane. Only resolved when Gloria's alibi cracks late in Round 3 and Marco admits seeing Gloria at the service stairwell.

**In-game texts:**
The Host/Detective or Destiny (the influencer) sends scripted texts to the group chat at key moments — the "we're done" breakup text, the Val/Paloma beach photos, Gloria's Spanish phone call overheard. These are evidence drops delivered via real text message to keep the game feeling modern and immersive.

## 2. CHARACTER DOSSIER REVISIONS

### Format
Each dossier simplified to ~150-250 words:
- **Name / Role / Age** (one line)
- **Who you are** (2-3 sentences — public persona)
- **Your 3 secrets** (numbered, 1-2 sentences each — no exact dollar amounts)
- **Your alibi** (2-3 sentences)
- **Your connections** (bullet list, 1 line each)
- **Your props** (physical items you carry)

### Character changes

**Paloma Rathbone — now female**
- Played by Dina (cooking, so she's "dead")
- Russian-born offshore tax attorney turned tax influencer
- Charming, manipulative, handsy drunk — uses intimacy as currency and alcohol as an excuse
- Had affairs with both Val and Pam (both women are bi/queer)
- Estranged spouse back in Russia
- Body proxy: stuffed clothes in laundry basket

**Fiona Beaumont** (was Fletcher/Fletch)
- Female photographer, broken wrist from surfing
- For Dina's friend Ira

**Olga Davenport** (was Chip)
- Female retired lawyer, late 50s (not 70s, no walker)
- Sharp, mobile, well-preserved
- Eastern European background — knew Paloma from the Moscow days

**Rosa Martinez** — Night Manager NPC
- Female, supporting NPC
- Reduced role since Host is now the Detective (Remy Marchand / Shane)

**Diego Reyes — ghost**
- Playable as a spectral/flashback presence
- Round cards reveal his backstory progressively

### 3 secrets per character — simplified (no exact amounts)

**Val Montecito:**
1. She runs a trafficking/escort operation through the hotel to keep it financially afloat
2. Paloma was blackmailing her — she'd been paying hush money to keep the operation quiet
3. Her father threatened to cut off her trust fund if the hotel fails — the trafficking is the only thing keeping her solvent

**Marco Reyes:**
1. Gloria is his mother (no one at the party knows this)
2. He has a go-bag with cash and a fake passport — he's planning to disappear
3. Paloma's bad intelligence got his brother Diego killed two years ago

**Zane Nakamura:**
1. Paloma deliberately destroyed his crypto hardware wallet (the margarita spills were sabotage) — a fortune in trafficking proceeds is gone
2. He owes Gloria a massive debt and is behind on payments — she'll destroy him if she finds out the wallet is gone
3. He's painfully in love with Val, but she sees him as a tool

**Dex Calloway:**
1. Real name Dexter Villareal — old money family he disowned
2. He spent years as a cartel fixer for Gloria's operation — she still has leverage over him
3. He's secretly in love with Irina — doesn't know about her and Marco

**Gloria Reyes:**
1. She runs a cartel operation spanning Florida and the Caribbean — the soccer mom thing is a cover
2. She ordered Irina to poison Paloma's shirt through the hotel laundry
3. Her son Diego was killed two years ago because of Paloma's bad intelligence — she has been planning revenge ever since

**Pam Worthington:**
1. Her first husband died under suspicious circumstances — she was blackout drunk and terrified she killed him
2. She blacked out again tonight and woke up near the laundry room with a vial of chemical residue — she doesn't know how she got there
3. Paloma made a cruel comment about her the night before — she was furious and told people she'd "make her pay"

**Doug Kaminski:**
1. His porta potty business is a money-laundering front for Gloria's cartel
2. He wasn't in Pittsburgh — he arrived at the hotel through the service entrance hours before his "official" arrival
3. He has a protective crush on Val and would do almost anything to keep her out of trouble

**Irina Volkov:**
1. She's Gloria's operative — not just a housekeeper, a handler
2. Gloria ordered her to prepare and deliver the poisoned shirt — she did it
3. Paloma was the only person kind to her — spoke Russian, brought her poetry. She helped kill the one person who saw her.

**Sunny Patel:**
1. Not a consultant — undercover IRS/Interpol agent embedded at the conference
2. Paloma was her star informant, set to testify soon — the death destroys her career and years of investigation
3. She misspent federal expense money on personal luxuries instead of witness security — if discovered, she's done

## 4. ROUND CARDS

Each player gets a physical card at each round's start. Format:
- Round timestamp (matches flipboard)
- 1-2 sentence scene-setter
- 3-4 talking points (what to say, who to engage, what to watch for)
- Optional secret action (send a text, pass a note, confront someone)

Written in second person. Host/Detective gets a master card per round with all beats and NPC lines.
