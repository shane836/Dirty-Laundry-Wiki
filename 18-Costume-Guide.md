## COSTUME GUIDE

Costume suggestions for each character in **Dirty Laundry: Murder in the Caymans**. The setting is a boutique hotel in the Grand Caymans during the after-party of an exclusive "Tax Optimization Seminar." Think tropical noir — resort wear with an edge.

Send this guide to your players ahead of the party so they can dress the part. No plot details are included.

---

## Major Characters (Suspects)

---

### Val Montecito — The Hotel Owner
**Female | Early 30s**

The woman who owns the hotel and looks like she was born poolside with a champagne flute. Designer everything, effortlessly glamorous, never underdressed.

**Costume:**
- Silk or satin slip dress, tropical print or solid jewel tone
- Gold jewelry — layered necklaces, statement earrings, bangles
- Strappy heels or embellished sandals
- Oversized designer sunglasses pushed up on head
- Champagne glass as permanent accessory
- Fresh tan (real or sprayed)
- Hair: beachy waves, effortless but clearly took an hour

**Vibe:** If a luxury resort ad came to life and started ordering people around.

---

### Marco Reyes — The Valet
**Male | Early 20s**

The hotel's head valet. Young, telenovela-handsome, charming with every guest. The kind of guy who remembers your drink order and your dog's name.

**Costume:**
- Hotel uniform: black or white button-down, sleeves rolled to elbows
- Dark slacks, polished shoes
- Simple watch or bracelet
- Valet name tag (make one — "MARCO" on a hotel-branded tag)
- Car keys on a lanyard or key hook clipped to belt
- Hair: styled but not fussy

**Vibe:** The guy in the telenovela who's too pretty to be "just the help."

---

### Zane Nakamura — The Crypto Hustler
**Male | Late 20s**

The tech bro who won't stop talking about blockchain, AI, and "disruption." Made money young and wants everyone to know it.

**Costume:**
- Expensive streetwear: designer hoodie or graphic tee under a blazer, or an **"OnlyTans"** branded t-shirt (his "tanning salon" business — great prop)
- Slim joggers or tailored shorts
- Flashy sneakers (Yeezys, Jordans, whatever's loud)
- Rolex or oversized smart watch
- Phone in hand at all times (non-negotiable)
- AirPods in one ear
- Optional: crypto/Web3 lanyard or conference badge

**Vibe:** The guy at the pool who's "closing a deal" on his phone while ordering a $40 smoothie.

---

### Dex Calloway — The Bartender
**Male | Mid 30s**

The bartender who's way too smart and way too well-read to be "just a bartender." Makes perfect cocktails. Quotes philosophers between pours. Dangerously good listener.

**Costume:**
- Clean white button-down or henley, sleeves rolled
- Dark apron (bar-style, waist-tied)
- Dark jeans or slacks
- Bar towel over shoulder
- Simple leather watch or wrist wrap
- Optional: cocktail shaker or bar tools tucked in apron
- Hair: effortlessly together, nothing flashy

**Vibe:** The bartender in a movie who clearly has a past but won't tell you about it unless you order neat bourbon.

---

### Gloria "Glo" Reyes — The Consultant
**Female | Late 40s**

Here representing a "shell company." Looks like a soccer mom on vacation — linen, blowout, perfect manners. Chairs charity committees. Brings deviled eggs to potlucks. Radiates PTA president energy.

**Costume:**
- Crisp linen blouse or wrap top
- Tailored resort pants or a tasteful midi skirt
- Wedge sandals or nice flats
- Pearl or gold stud earrings
- Designer tote bag or structured clutch
- Reading glasses on a chain (optional but *chef's kiss*)
- Hair: excellent blowout, not a strand out of place

**Vibe:** The mom at the country club who's somehow on every board and committee simultaneously.

---

### Pam Worthington — The Divorcee
**Female | Mid 40s**

Came to the Caymans to "find herself" but really to find a rich second husband. Resort wear that's trying a little too hard. Jewelry that announces recent alimony.

**Costume:**
- Bold resort dress or jumpsuit — tropical print, bright colors
- Statement jewelry: oversized cocktail ring, chunky bracelet, chandelier earrings
- Wedge heels or platform sandals
- Large sun hat or fascinator (optional, but very Pam)
- Wine glass or cocktail as permanent accessory
- Full makeup, maybe slightly smudged as the evening goes on
- Designer handbag she wants you to notice

**Vibe:** Real Housewives energy at a Caribbean resort. Fabulous and slightly unhinged.

---

### Doug "The Duke" Kaminski — The Porta Potty King
**Male | Early 50s**

Boisterous Pittsburgh businessman who runs a porta potty rental empire. Big man, big laugh, big presence. Self-nicknamed "The Duke." Makes porta potty jokes constantly and never tires of them.

**Costume:**
- Hawaiian shirt (loud, unapologetic) or a golf polo
- Khaki shorts or dad-length cargo shorts
- White sneakers or boat shoes
- Pittsburgh Steelers merchandise — hat, pin, watch band, anything
- Cigar (unlit prop)
- Business card holder he pulls out constantly ("Kaminski's Portable Sanitation — KPS")
- Optional: fanny pack

**Vibe:** The guy at every corporate retreat who calls himself "The Duke," slaps backs, and insists he's picking up the tab.

---

### Irina Volkov — The Housekeeper
**Female | Around 50**

The hotel's head of housekeeping. Quiet, efficient, everywhere and nowhere. Speaks with a Russian accent. Guests forget she exists ten minutes after meeting her.

**Costume:**
- Simple hotel housekeeping uniform: black or gray polo/blouse with dark pants or skirt
- Flat comfortable shoes (she's on her feet all day)
- Hotel ID badge on a lanyard
- Hair pulled back neatly — bun or low ponytail
- Minimal jewelry — maybe a small cross necklace or locket
- Master key card clipped to waist or lanyard
- Cleaning cloth tucked in pocket (optional prop)

**Vibe:** The woman who could walk through any room unnoticed — and that's exactly how she wants it.

---

### Dr. Sanjay "Sunny" Patel — The Consultant
**Any Gender | Early 40s**

The least memorable person at the conference. Management consultant from "Stratton Consulting Group." Talks in corporate buzzwords — "synergy," "optimization," "stakeholder alignment." Aggressively bland.

**Costume:**
- Forgettable business casual: khaki pants, blue or gray button-down
- Conference lanyard with "Stratton Consulting Group" badge (make one)
- Sensible shoes (loafers or oxfords)
- Leather portfolio or padfolio tucked under arm
- Pen in shirt pocket
- Plain watch
- Business cards from the fake consulting firm (fun prop to hand out)

**Vibe:** If a LinkedIn profile became a person. You've met them. You can't remember their face.

## Minor Characters (Optional — Not Suspects)

These characters add atmosphere and help draw out information during gameplay. The game works without them, but they deepen the experience.

---

### Fletcher "Fletch" Beaumont — The Conference Photographer
**Male | Around 30**

Hipster photographer hired to document the conference. Man-bun, considers himself a "visual storyteller." Has a broken wrist in a cast from a surfing accident.

**Costume:**
- Vintage or retro-print button-down, untucked
- Slim chinos or dark jeans
- Canvas sneakers or Birkenstocks
- Camera around neck (real or prop)
- Arm in a cast or wrist brace (dominant hand)
- Man-bun or messy topknot
- Optional: film camera, lens cap on a string, press pass

**Vibe:** The guy at every event who calls photography "capturing moments" and definitely has a portfolio website.

---

### Destiny Rivers — The Influencer
**Female | Early 20s**

Instagram and TikTok influencer invited as the conference's "content partner." Phone battery always at 3%. Ring light energy.

**Costume:**
- Trendy outfit: crop top and high-waisted pants, or mini dress
- Platform sneakers or trendy boots
- Phone in hand at ALL times (non-negotiable)
- Ring light or selfie stick (great prop)
- Oversized sunglasses
- Heavy accessorizing: layered chains, scrunchie on wrist
- Full glam makeup
- Optional: portable charger dangling from bag

**Vibe:** She's live-streaming right now. You're in the background of her Story. She did not ask permission.

---

### Chip Davenport — The Retired Lawyer
**Male | Around 70**

Retired tax attorney who "wrote the book on offshore trusts." Attends this conference annually for the free drinks. Has been coming for 30 years. Basically furniture.

**Costume:**
- Linen blazer or seersucker sport coat
- Polo shirt or open-collar dress shirt
- Light slacks
- Loafers, no socks (old money move)
- Walker or cane
- Glasses on a chain
- Old-fashioned cocktail as permanent accessory
- Optional: pocket square, signet ring

**Vibe:** The grandpa who's seen everything, judged everyone, and is only here for the top-shelf scotch.

---

### Reverend James "Jimmy" Whitfield — The Conference Chaplain
**Male | Early 60s**

Non-denominational chaplain hired by the conference. Runs a confessional booth by the pool. Takes "notes for his memoir." Silver fox energy.

**Costume:**
- Dog collar (clerical collar) paired with a Hawaiian shirt — this combo is essential
- Linen pants or chinos
- Sandals or casual loafers
- Small notebook and pen (for "memoir notes")
- Cross necklace or lapel pin
- Reading glasses
- Optional: Bible or devotional tucked under arm

**Vibe:** Jimmy Buffett meets the clergy. Spiritual advisor to the morally bankrupt, and loving every minute.

---

## General Costume Tips

**For the host:** Dress as the hotel concierge or event coordinator — neutral, authoritative, slightly harried. Black outfit with a clipboard works great.

**Props matter:** Business cards, lanyards, hotel key cards, and drink accessories go a long way toward selling the setting. Print fake conference badges for "Tax Optimization Seminar — Heathens Hollow, Grand Cayman."

**Tone reminder:** This is tropical noir. Everyone should look like they belong at an upscale Caribbean resort where something is slightly off. Sun-kissed and suspicious.
