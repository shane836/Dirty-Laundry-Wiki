# Tomás Herrera — The Maintenance Guy

**Male | 40s | MINOR CHARACTER (Not a suspect)**

---

## Who You Are

Hotel maintenance. You fix things — plumbing, electrical, generators, whatever breaks. You were supposed to be off-island tonight, but a generator on the far side of the property needed emergency repair. You've been out there all evening and only came in when you heard the commotion. You know this building better than anyone, including every service corridor, stairwell, and shortcut.

## Why You're Not a Suspect

You were on the far side of the property fixing a generator from 6 PM onward. Maintenance logs and your location (physically removed from the hotel buildings) clear you. You only returned to the main area after 11 PM.

## What You Know (reveal through conversation)

- **The service stairwell side exit was propped open** since about 6 PM. Someone wedged a doorstop in it. "That door is supposed to be locked. Fire code. Somebody wanted it open on purpose."
- You noticed **chemical supplies missing from the maintenance closet** two days ago. Cleaning compounds — the kind that could be repurposed. "I filed a report with Rosa. Nobody followed up."
- You saw **Irina carrying a garment bag at 7 PM** and thought it was odd. "She does laundry, not room deliveries. That's a different department."
- You have a **master key that's supposed to be checked in** when you're off-duty, but you forgot to return it. "It's in my toolbox. I didn't use it tonight — I was out by the generator. But yes, technically there's one more master key floating around than Rosa's count shows."

## Questions You Can Ask

- *To Rosa/Detective:* "Did anybody check who propped that service door open? Because that doesn't happen by accident."
- *To Irina:* "Irina, since when do you do garment deliveries? That's not your job."
- *To the room:* "I should mention — there's one more master key than the log shows. Mine. It was in my toolbox the whole time, but I can't prove that."

## Your Props

Work shirt, tool belt or wrench, master key (the extra one)
