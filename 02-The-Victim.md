## SECTION 3: THE VICTIM

### Richard "Richie" Rathbone | The Tax Influencer

#### Physical Profile
Middle-aged. The kind of middle-aged that's been preserved by expensive vodka, Caribbean sun, and the confidence of a man who's never been told no by anyone who matters.

#### Public Profile
- Conference star attraction
- The expert you called when you wanted to move money somewhere the government couldn't follow
- So good he became a **"tax influencer"**
- Excellent conversationalist
- Drinks expensive vodka
- Known for his charm, his connections, his ability to make the impossible sound routine

#### The Secret
Two weeks ago, Richie decided he was tired of helping bad people do worse things.

He reached out to **Dr. Sanjay Patel**—who he believed was a legitimate management consultant—but who was actually an **undercover IRS/Interpol agent**. They made a deal:
- Richie would flip and provide evidence against everyone at the conference
- He would walk away clean
- He would start a new life in witness protection

**The Bitter Irony:** The conference was supposed to be his last hurrah. Instead, it became his tomb.

### What People Know
- Excellent conversationalist; drinks expensive Russian vodka
- Recent divorce rumors (unconfirmed but whispered about constantly)
- Has a wife (somewhere in Moscow?) and at least one ex-mistress
  - **Val Montecito** — the hotel owner, recent affair (recently ended)
- Made a lot of people very rich
- Was making a speech tonight that **"nobody wanted to miss"**
- Generous with money—lent Val $500K to renovate the hotel
- Been spending lavishly lately, even by his standards
- Always has time for everyone, always knows what to say
- No visible enemies (which is suspicious in itself)

### What Could Be Discovered
**Law Enforcement Connection:**
- He was meeting with an IRS/Interpol agent (Sunny Patel)
- He kept encrypted files on a laptop containing **evidence on everyone**
- Federal expense money ($50K) was being spent on personal luxuries

**Personal Life:**
- His marriage was on the rocks—wife in Moscow hasn't spoken to him in months
- His affair with Val ended badly—he dumped her when he decided to flip
- He owed money to several **dangerous people**—and several dangerous people owed money to him

**Criminal Connections:**
- He was implicated in the death of one of **Gloria's children** (bad intel led to a fatal cartel ambush)
- He embezzled from **Zane's crypto wallet** (the "margarita spill" was deliberate sabotage)

**Financial Obligations:**
- He lent Val $500K and was about to call in the debt—money she doesn't have
- Multiple IOUs floating around in the offshore finance world
- Several "friends" who were about to become very wealthy from his betrayal—unless they stopped him first

### Why Richie Was the Perfect Victim
He was useful, then he became a threat. He was generous, then he became a liability. He was connected to everyone in the room, which meant when he talked, **everyone would burn**.
