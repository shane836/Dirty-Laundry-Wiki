# Setting and Premise

## DIRTY LAUNDRY: MURDER IN THE CAYMANS

**A Murder Mystery Dinner Party Game**

**Players:** 9 Main Suspects + 1 Victim + 1 Ghost + up to 10 Minor Characters + 1 Host (The Mentalist)

**Logline:** When an offshore tax attorney organizing a conference in the Caymans threatens to blow the whistle on everyone's dirty deals, somebody decides her laundry — literal and figurative — needs to be aired permanently.

**Tone:** Tropical noir. Succession meets Weeds on a Caribbean beach. Everyone's smiling, everyone's guilty, everyone's lying.

**Theme:** Money makes people beautiful and terrible. When everybody's got secrets, anybody could be the killer.

---

## The Setting: "Heathens Hollow"

A boutique hotel in the Grand Cayman Islands, present day. Hosting the tail end of an exclusive "Tax Optimization Seminar" — really a networking event for wealthy people looking to hide money and avoid accountability.

The conference was organized by **Paloma Rathbone**, a Russian-born offshore tax attorney turned tax influencer.

**The physical space (mapped to venue):**
- **Outdoor area (yard/pool):** Courtyard where the after-party takes place. Steel drum music, string lights, a glowing pool. Round 1 happens here.
- **Main structure:** The hotel lobby, bar, and conference rooms. Has a front entrance and a side entrance. The side entrance doubles as the "service stairwell" — the passage Gloria uses. Rounds 2-4 happen here.
- **Side structure:** The "service area" including the laundry room where the body is found. This is the crime scene.

**Atmosphere:** Paradise that feels slightly sinister. Cocktails, Caribbean ambiance, and the energy of people who just spent three days learning new ways to commit white-collar crime.

---

## The Night of the Murder

It's the after-party following the conference's closing dinner. Paloma was supposed to give a final toast at 9 PM — something she described as "something nobody will want to miss." She never showed.

The body was discovered face-down in a laundry basket in the service area, with poison residue on the collar of her dress shirt.

Everyone was at the conference. Everyone had opportunity. Everyone had motive.

---

## How the Game Works

**4 rounds** of structured roleplay. Each round has a printed timestamp displayed on a flipboard.

| Round | Title | In-Game Time | Real Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The After-Party" | 8:00 – 10:00 PM | ~30 min |
| 2 | "Everyone's a Suspect" | 10:00 – 11:00 PM | ~30 min |
| 3 | "The Investigation" | 11:00 PM – Midnight | ~45 min |
| 4 | "The Reveal" | Midnight | ~15 min |

**Each player receives:**
- A **character dossier** (who you are, your 3 secrets, your alibi, your connections)
- A **round card** at the start of each round (3-4 bullet points telling you what to say, who to engage, what to watch for)
- Any **character props** listed in their dossier

**The Host** plays Fortunado Marchand, "The Mentalist" — a stage mind-reader hired as the evening's entertainment. When the body drops, he takes command of the room: reading faces, pressing for tells, controlling pacing, delivering evidence, interrogating characters, and making dramatic announcements.

**The goal:** Figure out who killed Paloma Rathbone. Players vote at the end of Round 4.
